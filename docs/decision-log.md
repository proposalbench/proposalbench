# Development decision log

ProposalBench is built by Proposia, whose product is evaluated as one participant. This log records,
as they happened, the decisions that could favour or disfavour any participant. It was kept in a
private repository during development and is published with v1.0. Individuals' names and account
details are removed (decisions are attributed to the maintainers); the rules and their reasons are
unchanged.

## Participant versions

| System | Version | Notes |
|---|---|---|

Proposia is closed source: its outputs and judgments are released, its generation cannot be rerun outside Proposia. |

An earlier Proposia version (commit `e2fe2a9`, pinned on 2026-10-30) was run during development. Its
results are not reported. The rule recorded with that pin, that no change to Proposia motivated by
ProposalBench would be made before a sealed run, was superseded on 2026-11-05: Pro v2 was developed
against the public split, and no sealed run is part of v1.0.

## Protocol

1. All evaluator development uses the pilot and public splits only.
2. v1.0 publishes the public split (60 tasks), the rubrics, the scoring specification, the judge
   panel, the prompts, the reference agent, every generated proposal and every judgment.
3. A sealed split, authored after the evaluator and released only as file hashes until it is run,
   is planned for a later version; it is not part of v1.0.

|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-30 | Six schemes for v1 (Horizon Europe RIA, ERC StG, MSCA PF, NIH R21, NSF, AEI in Spanish); Erasmus+ KA220, CERV, UKRI, DFG deferred. | Depth of validation before breadth; UKRI text is CC BY-NC-SA, incompatible with CC BY data. |
| 2026-10-30 | One judge panel for every system, drawn from model families no compared system uses. | Per-system judge exclusion makes scores incomparable; Proposia uses Google, Anthropic and Qwen models. |
| 2026-10-30 | AI-style ("slop") metrics are diagnostics, not part of the headline score. | Proposia's prompts target those patterns explicitly; they detect AI style rather than proposal quality. |
| 2026-10-30 | Integrity enters as fixed per-defect deductions; "not found" is never treated as "fabricated" without evidence. | Rate penalties can be diluted by padding; unresolvable references include legitimate books and reports. |
| 2026-10-30 | Number-only heading matches are reported as uncertain, not as present or missing. | A renamed section cannot be classified reliably by its number alone. |
| 2026-10-30 | Official template boilerplate (e.g. Horizon Europe's gender-dimension sentence) is allowlisted in the seed-copying check. | Every call of the scheme must carry it; it is not copying from a seed. |
| 2026-10-31 | Leniency-prone items (expected outcomes and scope elements, objectives, state of the art, methods, risks, work plan, impact pathway, dissemination) are split into atomic yes/no criteria; the item score is the share of criteria met, each criterion taking the median of valid judge answers (a 1-1 split counts 0.5). | Pilot: with single yes/partial/no verdicts, a 2,246-word Horizon Europe proposal (limit 18,000) scored 85 on quality; one-line mentions cleared "concrete mechanism". |
| 2026-10-31 | The state-of-the-art item counts only prior work by researchers other than the applicant, and adds a deterministic criterion: at least three verified external references. | Pilot: all three judges accepted the applicant's own earlier project as "the state of the art" in a proposal citing no external literature. |
| 2026-10-31 | Applicant-claim severity follows explicit rules (critical = the item does not exist or its substance differs; minor = small inaccuracy in a supported fact) and a consensus claim is critical only if most flagging judges call it critical. | Pilot: a misspelled co-author surname in an in-text citation was deducted as a critical fabrication. |
| 2026-10-31 | The judge instructions take a demanding-evaluator stance: a requirement is met only when the text gives the specific information. | Same pilot evidence of optimism bias, consistent with published findings on LLM judges. |
| 2026-10-31 | Judged criteria can carry a deterministic precondition (`requires`); the state-of-the-art criterion "discusses others' work with citations" requires at least one external reference. | Pilot: an 18,000-word proposal whose reference list held only the applicant's own papers had this criterion marked met by all judges. |
| 2026-10-31 | New deterministic item EV1 (evidence base): at least one external reference per 1,000 counted words and at least five verified external references. Thresholds are provisional and will be set from the funded anchor proposals before freeze. | NIH CSR lists missing relevant literature among the top problems; pilot direct baselines often cited no external literature. |
| 2026-10-31 | Plan coherence enters as deterministic item PC1 (substance), one criterion per fact-sheet check, unchecked items left out. | Whole-proposal inconsistencies (dates, budget, effort) are missed by section-level judging. |
| 2026-10-31 | Grounding items GR1-GR3 split into atomic criteria (specific prior results with findings, explicit link to aims, use of existing resources, dossier limitations answered, roles justified by stated expertise). | Pilot: grounding scored 100 for every sample under yes/partial/no verdicts, so it did not discriminate. |
| 2026-10-31 | Uncertainty intervals also resample samples within tasks. | Pilot: per-task means close together hid large sample-to-sample differences (48 vs 90 on one task). |
| 2026-10-31 | Reference verification never calls a reference MISATTRIBUTED (or its DOI wrong) when the raw reference text names the resolved work (title of 4+ words at partial ratio >= 95, or one of its first three author surnames), or when one title is the other without its subtitle; Pensoft/RIO references are parsed. | Anchor set: 222 of 598 real references were MISATTRIBUTED through parse errors (a -4 deduction each); after the fixes 0 of 598. |
| 2026-10-31 | NOT_FOUND stays neutral (no deduction) in v1. | Anchor set: 7.4% (95% CI 5.3-10.3%) of indexable real references not found (after the Crossref date fix), above the 2% bar. |
| 2026-10-31 | EV1 thresholds (first setting, superseded below): at least 2 external references per 1,000 counted words and at least 5 verified external references. | Anchor set: every anchor cites at least 2.27 references per 1,000 words (median 7.39); 10 of 11 have 11 or more verified references (the exception cites only web resources). |
| 2026-10-31 | OpenAlex is off unless OPENALEX_API_KEY is set; a server asking to wait more than 60 s makes its source unavailable for the rest of the run, and unavailable sources are recorded with each evaluation. | Keyless OpenAlex now has a small daily quota: it ran out mid-run (Retry-After 21 h) and made the source set depend on run order. |
| 2026-10-31 | `plan.activity_after_end` uses the call's maximum duration when no project duration is extracted. | A milestone after the call maximum is a defect whatever the proposal states; flaw injection found the gap. |
| 2026-10-31 | A DOI counts as unresolved on doi.org's 404 plus Crossref's 404 when OpenAlex was skipped for the run (no key or spent quota); a transient OpenAlex failure still blocks the verdict. | Flaw injection: with OpenAlex off, 0 of 17 fabricated references were flagged; after the change 18 of 18, with 0 of 18 false positives on controls citing a real paper. |
| 2026-10-31 | A paragraph contained in another (partial ratio >= 92, at least 60% of its length) counts as a duplicate. | Flaw injection: 2 of 19 duplicated paragraphs were missed because the original sat under a bold label line; after the change 19 of 19, 0 false positives. |
| 2026-10-31 | Applicant claims are pooled across judges and adjudicated: every judge rules on every candidate in one focused call; a majority of valid rulings decides, and fewer valid rulings than a majority is never a defect. | Flaw injection: two evaluations of near-identical texts differed by 7.3 points (SD), almost entirely through applicant-claim deductions appearing or vanishing; quality differed by only 1.4. Fabricated awards were detected 18 of 18 under both rules. |
| 2026-10-31 | An adjudicated applicant claim is critical only when every valid ruling says critical; a claim most rulings find unsupported but not unanimously critical is minor. | Re-test after adjudication: twin evaluations still differed by 7.4 points (SD), through 2-1 splits flipping a -10 deduction; with unanimity 4.4, while fabricated awards (unanimously critical) kept their full effect (+8.0 points of deduction against matched controls). |
| 2026-10-31 | Evidence-base counts (EV1, the G02 verified-reference check) use references cited in the text, at most three per citing sentence, and leave out references the panel finds unrelated to or contradicting their sentence. EV1 thresholds recalibrated on the anchors with this counting: at least 1 cited external reference per 1,000 counted words and at least 5 cited verified external references (10 of 11 anchors pass both; the exception cites only websites). | Flaw injection: appending ten irrelevant verified references cited in one sentence raised the score in 13 of 17 proposals, through EV1. After the change the mean effect is -2.2 points (median +0.8, within the 4.4-point evaluation noise); proposals without any literature can still gain on G02 when the panel finds the added works supportive. Reported as no reliable gain, not as invariance. |
| 2026-10-31 | New deterministic call item CALL-ST: one criterion per core required section of the call's template, met unless the section is reported missing (number-only matches count as present). | A missing required section was recorded but never scored; flaw injection showed that dropping a section did not reliably lower call responsiveness, since judges read the whole proposal when a section is absent. |
| 2026-10-31 | Undefined work packages and aims are also checked in the text: a prose reference to a WP/PT/Work Package or Aim numbered above the highest one defined (in a heading, table, bold label or line start) is a plan.undefined_wp deduction. Gaps below the highest number are not flagged. | Flaw injection: the fact-sheet route missed an "Aim 5" because two of three judges relabelled the aims WP1/WP2 and listed no references. The text check flags 13 of 13 injected references with 0 of 13 false positives on controls and none on the 19 pilot outputs or 11 anchors. |
| 2026-10-31 | Milestone and deliverable months are also checked in the text: a month beyond the call's maximum duration in a sentence that names or labels a milestone or deliverable, or in a table with a month or due-date column, is a plan.activity_after_end deduction. | Flaw injection: the fact-sheet route detected 2 of 5 injected late milestones (judges kept another mention of the same deliverable, or extracted no prose milestones). The text check detects 9 of 9 with no false positive on controls; on the pilot it found one real defect the sheet had missed (a 24-month R21 scheduling a milestone in month 30). |
| 2026-10-31 | The extracted fact sheet's undefined-work-package finding becomes a diagnostic (plan.undefined_wp_extracted); the deduction comes only from the text check. | Flaw injection: the sheet route both missed injected undefined references and fired on a control that renamed a work package consistently (1 of 13); the text check had 13 of 13 and 0 of 13. |
| 2026-10-31 | Presentation-neutral parsing: `__bold__` is treated like `**bold**` for headings, milestone labels and date cells; inline emphasis is removed from reference titles before searching; a run of two or more repeated sentences (25+ words) counts as a duplicate regardless of paragraph breaks; an author-year citation with a letter (2020a) matches only the reference with that letter. | Meaning-preserving invariance tests: each of these changed a deterministic finding under an edit that does not change content; after the fixes 0 of 50 transformed pilot outputs change any finding. |
| 2026-10-31 | Evidence-service request rates are scheduled per process, shared by all evaluations running in parallel. | Invariance tests: parallel evaluations each applied their own Crossref limit, triggering 429 responses that left identical references verified in one evaluation and not found in its twin. |
| 2026-10-31 | Prompt injection addressed to evaluators is treated as resisted: mean score change +1.7 (SD 7.2, n = 18) against a matched neutral insertion, within evaluation noise. Re-tested after the adjudication change: +0.6 (SD 4.9, n = 18). | Pre-declared invariance check (no detectable gain). |
