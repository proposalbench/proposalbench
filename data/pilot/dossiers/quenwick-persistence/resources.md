# Environment and resources

Bellwether Reach Biomedical Institute is a nonprofit research institute in North Carolina.
The following inventory is current on 30 October 2026.

## Available now

- Quenwick's laboratory has BSL-2 laboratory space, a plate reader, incubators, and access to
  a shared flow cytometer. The current institutional biosafety authorization covers the
  existing extracellular *S. aureus* methods work.
- A shared confocal microscope provides 6 reserved hours per week under the existing allocation.
  It has environmental control suitable for live-cell imaging. Routine core charges would
  need to be covered during the proposed award; the allocation is access, not unlimited
  prepaid usage. High-throughput automated screening is not available.
- PB-SA01 and PB-SA02 stocks and the versioned RESET-LAG analysis pipeline are held by the PI.
  The two isolates have identity and susceptibility records; fresh quality checks would be
  needed for the proposed work.
- Tessivar's laboratory has an authenticated THP-1 cell bank and mammalian cell-culture
  facilities. The current bank was STR-profiled and tested negative for mycoplasma in August
  2026. THP-1 is a leukemia-derived male-donor line, not a model of variation across donor sex
  or age. Sharing the bank and facilities within the institute is agreed.
- The institute maintains secure storage with daily backup, version control, a biosafety
  committee, and a human research protection office. Their existence does not constitute
  approval of the new study.
- Startup support covers 0.25 FTE of Pelvyn Norquill through December 2028. This allocation is
  already committed to the laboratory and cannot be counted as a new R21-funded hire.

## Proposed or conditional access

- Primary human monocytes from 8 adult donors are a planning option, not material in hand.
  A commercial supplier has provided an indicative quote and can ordinarily supply recorded
  donor sex and age bands with nonidentifiable samples. No order, reserved lots, or guaranteed
  donor balance exists. The final sample size must be scientifically justified and affordable.
- No patient cohort or clinical recruitment agreement exists. The institute's human research
  protection office would assess the proposed source and identifiability of any human
  material; no project-specific determination has been issued.
- Adding infected mammalian-cell experiments requires a biosafety amendment and training.
  The amendment has not been submitted. The team anticipates an early setup stage but cannot
  guarantee an approval date.
- An additional 0.50 FTE research assistant is a proposed hire contingent on funding.
  No candidate has been selected. Core training and additional microscope time would require
  scheduling and payment.
- Intracellular antibiotic quantification by LC-MS is not an established capability of this
  team. A fee-for-service assay might be explored, but no validated assay or service agreement
  is in place. Designs relying on it must address the dependency or use a justified alternative.

There is no infected-animal facility allocation for this project. The lab can pursue a bounded
cell-based study using the existing core, but cannot claim an established animal infection
platform or a completed primary-cell validation campaign.
