## Position and preparation

Dr. Veylora Quenwick is Assistant Professor of Microbial Systems at Bellwether Reach Biomedical Institute, North Carolina, since September 2023.
The institute is a fictional US nonprofit biomedical research organization. She leads a small
laboratory investigating heterogeneity in bacterial responses to antibiotics.

All institutions named here are fictional. The PhD studied variability in growth recovery
after nutrient stress. Her postdoctoral work developed image-analysis methods for separating
bacterial aggregates from individual objects and comparing image counts with culture-based
measurements.

## Personal statement

My contribution is quantitative bacterial microscopy linked to interpretable measures of
survival. I can lead assay development, image analysis, and careful comparisons of broth
phenotypes. My laboratory has not yet studied infected macrophages. Moving into host-cell
biology requires collaboration, training, and a narrower question than a comprehensive account
of recurrent infection. I want to establish whether my measurement skills can reveal a useful
host-context effect, while keeping the distinction between an assay signal and a biological
mechanism explicit.
