# Consortium: partners, people and resources

> The applicant, partners, people, grants and publications in this dossier are fictional and
> were created for ProposalBench.

The coordinator has agreed the following partnership for a Horizon Europe proposal. Roles are
the partners' own descriptions of what they can contribute; the work plan is still open.

## 1. Instituto de Agroecología del Bajo Ebro (IABE), Spain: coordinator

Public research institute, 64 staff, part of a regional agri-food research network.

- **Dr. Lucía Arnedo Velasco** (coordinator; see `cv_coordinator.md`).
- **Dr. Tomás Gil Abenoza**, senior researcher in soil physics; leads the IABE long-term
  experiments at Alcañiz, Caspe and Maella.
- **Javier Sopena Lahoz** and **Marta Ibarz Cuello**, PhD candidates (COVERAGUA).
- Resources: the three long-term experiments (est. 2009, 2014, 2016); 96 capacitance soil
  moisture probes already installed; a soil physics laboratory with pressure-plate apparatus;
  the COVERAGUA dataset (3.1 million records).
- Experience in EU projects: partner in one Horizon 2020 project (work-package leader for field
  trials); never coordinated an EU project.

## 2. Aegean Institute for Soil and Water (AISW), Greece

Research centre of a Greek agricultural university, 38 staff.

- **Dr. Eleni Karvounaki**, remote sensing and soil-water modelling; developed a
  Sentinel-2-based soil moisture downscaling method for Thessaly (see `publications.md`).
- **Dr. Stavros Melidonis**, agronomist; runs a network of 15 olive and cotton farms used for
  on-farm trials.
- Resources: high-performance computing allocation; 6 years of eddy-covariance data from one
  olive orchard in Thessaly.

## 3. Universidade de Montalvo (UMON), Portugal

University; the partner is its Centre for Rural Economics.

- **Prof. Rui Valadares Pinho**, agricultural economist; specialist in farm-level cost–benefit
  analysis and in the design of CAP eco-schemes; adviser to a national CAP strategic plan working
  group from 2022 to 2024.
- **Dr. Inês Moutinho Rebelo**, rural sociologist; works on gender and decision-making in family
  farms.
- Resources: a panel survey of 410 Alentejo farms (2019–2024).

## 4. Fondazione Rurale Dauna (FRD), Italy

Non-profit foundation in Apulia that runs advisory services and farmer field schools.

- **Dr. Chiara Lombardozzi**, head of advisory services; coordinates 22 advisors.
- Resources: access to 40 durum wheat and olive farms on vertisols; a demonstration farm.

## 5. Humidea Srl, Italy (SME)

Software SME, 14 employees, develops irrigation scheduling apps used on 1,200 farms in Italy and
Spain.

- **Ing. Davide Sorrentino**, chief technology officer.
- Interests: would like to turn research outputs into a commercial decision-support service.
  The company has also asked whether the project could fund development of a new low-cost
  soil-moisture sensor of its own design.

## 6. Cooperativa Agraria Llanos del Cierzo (CALC), Spain

Farmer cooperative, 340 member farms (cereal, almond, olive), 31% of them managed by women.

- **Pilar Royo Embid**, technical manager.
- Role: hosts on-farm trials, recruits farmers, runs farmer workshops; 5 of the 12 COVERAGUA farms
  are CALC members.

## Other relevant links

- Two of the three long-term experiments are part of a European network of agricultural long-term
  experiments, which allows data sharing with sites in other countries.
- IABE and FRD took part in the same Horizon 2020 thematic network on agroecology (2019–2022) and
  know each other's advisory work.
