# Notes from the coordinator

We want to scale up what we learned in COVERAGUA to the whole Mediterranean with the partners in
`consortium.md`. Our idea, in short:

- Test cover-crop termination rules and reduced tillage on many more farms and soils (Greece,
  Portugal, southern Italy), not just in our region.
- Turn our termination-date spreadsheet into a proper decision-support tool linked to weather
  forecasts and satellite soil moisture, together with Humidea.
- Finally bring in economists and social scientists, which was our weak point.

Practicalities:

- We were thinking of asking for about EUR 6.5 million so that every partner has enough.
- Humidea would like a work package to develop their own new low-cost soil-moisture sensor; it
  would help them commercially and keep them motivated.
- 48 months.
