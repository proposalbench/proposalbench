# MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships — European Fellowship — PB-MSCA-PF-2027-EF

> This call is fictional and was written for ProposalBench. Scheme rules are adapted from
> the amended MSCA Work Programme 2026–2027 and the Part B template. The submission date
> and the restrictions expressly identified below are invented. The research topic is
> freely chosen. Submit Part B-1 in English; this language restriction is a benchmark choice.

## Purpose and award conditions

Support excellent postdoctoral research through international mobility, individual training,
supervision and reciprocal knowledge transfer. The fellowship should strengthen the
researcher's skills and career opportunities and the participating organisations' capacity.

| Condition | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Variant | European Fellowship |
| Duration | 12–24 months at the beneficiary |
| Beneficiary | One independent legal entity in an EU Member State or a country associated to MSCA; it employs and supervises the fellow |
| Funding | MSCA unit contributions, determined by eligible months, country correction and applicable allowances; no generic fixed monetary cap |
| Fictional submission deadline | 15 September 2027 (invented) |
| Deliverable | Part B-1, sections 1–3 only; no monetary budget narrative |

The researcher must hold a PhD by the deadline; an unconditional successful defence before
the deadline also qualifies even if the degree has not yet been formally awarded. Research
experience must be at most eight full-time-equivalent years since the PhD award. Exclude
periods outside research and career breaks. Apply the documented parental-leave deductions
in the work programme, including 18 months per child for maternity unless longer leave
is documented. The research-experience exemption for qualifying reintegration to Europe
applies only when its nationality/residence and recent-return conditions are met; it is
not a blanket exemption for any overseas research.

For mobility, the researcher must not have resided or carried out their main activity in
the country of the beneficiary for more than 12 months during the 36 months immediately
before the deadline. Main activity includes work and study; assess both physical location
and the country of the institution for which the activity was performed. Compulsory
national service, short holidays, and time in refugee-status or EU temporary-protection
procedures are excluded. Researchers holding the qualifying refugee status or EU temporary
protection at the deadline are exempt from mobility. For the international organisations
specified in the work programme, the equivalent test concerns the appointing organisation.
Describe the residence and main-activity history relevant to the deadline and identify
any periods requiring confirmation.

European Fellowships are open to researchers of any nationality. The beneficiary hosts
the standard fellowship in the EU or a country associated to MSCA.

## Country status used by this fictional call

As verified on **31 October 2026**, the countries associated for the MSCA part of Horizon
Europe are **Albania, Armenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Egypt, Faroe Islands, Georgia,
Iceland, Israel, Kosovo, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Norway, Serbia, Switzerland,
Tunisia, Türkiye, Ukraine and the United Kingdom**. The Kosovo designation is without
prejudice to positions on status. These countries are **not non-associated third countries
for a Global Fellowship outgoing phase**. In particular, Switzerland cannot be the
outgoing country; geographical location outside the EU does not make it eligible for that phase.

Canada, Japan, New Zealand and the Republic of Korea are associated only to Pillar II,
not MSCA, so they remain non-associated for this fellowship and can host a Global outgoing
phase. The Commission list separately records transitional arrangements for Morocco and, for
Pillar II in 2027, Australia; it does not list those arrangements as effective association
agreements. For this fictional call, use the effective-association snapshot above. A
proposal relying on a transitional arrangement must identify that issue for verification
rather than assert full association.

For research covered by Euratom, the beneficiary must be in an EU Member State or a
country associated to the Euratom Research and Training Programme, and the fellow must
be a national or qualifying long-term resident of such a country. The ordinary MSCA
country list alone does not establish Euratom eligibility.

## Secondments and fellowship funding

Optional secondments must add identifiable research or training value. For a European
Fellowship they may occupy at most half the standard fellowship duration. For a Global
Fellowship they may occupy at most half the outgoing phase; up to three months can be at
the beneficiary at the start and count within that outgoing phase. No secondment may occur
during the mandatory return phase.

The real scheme can additionally fund up to six months in a non-academic organisation
in the EU or an eligible Associated Country at the end. **This fictional standard-duration
variant excludes that additional placement (invented restriction)**. Its duration ceiling
therefore remains 24 months. Explain the work, effort and resource access in Part B-1;
financial unit-contribution forms are outside this narrative task.

## Expected outcomes

- A broader set of research and transferable skills, with a credible improvement in the fellow’s career prospects inside or outside academia.
- New research approaches and stronger professional networks through international mobility, with interdisciplinary or intersectoral learning where relevant.
- Stronger host research and training capacity through reciprocal knowledge transfer, with opportunities to feed research results into teaching and wider communication.

## Scope

- An investigator-chosen research and training project, submitted jointly by one postdoctoral researcher and one beneficiary, under the European Fellowship variant.

## Exclusions

- Ineligible mobility: more than 12 months of residence or main activity in the relevant host country during the 36 months immediately before the fictional deadline, unless a documented work-programme exemption applies.
- An additional end-of-project non-academic placement is outside this benchmark variant (an invented restriction); ordinary secondments within the fellowship remain possible.

## Mandatory elements

- State the PhD status, relevant research-experience calculation and mobility history at the fictional deadline, identifying periods or documents requiring confirmation.
- Identify the beneficiary and responsible supervisors.
- Describe access to the required hosting resources.
- Specify individual training and supervision arrangements.
- Define reciprocal knowledge-transfer activities.
- Plan the researcher’s Career Development Plan jointly with the supervisor and schedule it as a deliverable at the beginning of the action.
- Present a timed work plan with responsibilities and effort, including all host stays and secondments.
- Provide a Gantt chart locating milestones and deliverables.
- Identify implementation risks and specific mitigations.
- Define measures to develop the fellow’s career.
- Plan dissemination of research outputs to scientific audiences.
- Plan exploitation of results by intended users.
- Plan communication with non-specialist audiences.
- Explain intellectual-property management, or justify its non-applicability.
- Specify a 12–24-month fellowship at the beneficiary in an EU Member State or country associated to MSCA.

## Required narrative: Part B-1

Use the following numbered headings. Part B-2, CV attachments, organisational capacity
forms and commitment letters are separate in the real application and are not requested
as additional text here.

### 1 Excellence

Present the research, training and reciprocal transfer of knowledge as a coherent fellowship.

#### 1.1 Quality and pertinence of the project's research and innovation objectives

State specific, assessable objectives and explain their ambition relative to the state of the art.

#### 1.2 Soundness of the proposed methodology

Explain the research design, methods and analysis, interdisciplinary contributions, relevant sex/gender and other diversity dimensions, and open-science practices. Justify non-applicability where appropriate; address data management and ethical implications in relation to the actual methods.

#### 1.3 Quality of the supervision, training and of the two-way transfer of knowledge between the researcher and the host

Define individual scientific and transferable-skills training, its providers, supervision and feedback arrangements, and knowledge flowing in both directions. For Global Fellowships, explain transfer from the outgoing host into the return host through specific return-phase activities. Plan a joint Career Development Plan as a deliverable at the beginning of the fellowship and review it as needs change.

#### 1.4 Quality and appropriateness of the researcher's professional experience, competences and skills

Connect relevant expertise and experience to the work and training needs. Distinguish published work from work under review and proposed activities from past achievements.

### 2 Impact

Explain benefits for the researcher, host organisations and users of the research.

#### 2.1 Credibility of the measures to enhance the career perspectives and employability of the researcher and contribution to their skills development

Identify an intended career direction and the specific skills, experience and networks that improve access to it, within or beyond academia.

#### 2.2 Suitability and quality of the measures to maximise expected outcomes and impacts, as set out in the dissemination and exploitation plan, including communication

Specify dissemination to research audiences, exploitation by intended users and communication to wider publics, with timing and indicators. Explain protection, licensing or open release where intellectual-property decisions matter, or justify their irrelevance.

#### 2.3 The magnitude and importance of the project's contribution to the expected scientific, societal and economic impacts

Explain the scale, importance and plausible pathways of the scientific and, where relevant, societal and economic contributions without unsupported numerical promises.

### 3 Quality and efficiency of the implementation

Demonstrate that the work and individual development fit the fellowship duration and hosting arrangements.

#### 3.1 Quality and effectiveness of the work plan, assessment of risks and appropriateness of the effort assigned to work packages

Provide work packages, responsibilities, effort, milestones, deliverables, a Gantt chart and risks with mitigation. Show training, dissemination and any secondments on the schedule; for a Global Fellowship show the entire outgoing and return phases.

#### 3.2 Quality and capacity of the host institutions and participating organisations, including hosting arrangements

Explain access to facilities, integration into each host team, administrative and relocation support, and the contribution of each participating organisation. For Global Fellowships cover both hosts and the transition. Distinguish documented commitments from arrangements still to be secured.

## Review criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Main focus |
|---|---|---|
| Excellence | 50% | Objectives, methodology, supervision, training, knowledge transfer and researcher experience |
| Impact | 30% | Career prospects, dissemination, exploitation, communication and expected contributions |
| Quality and efficiency of the implementation | 20% | Work plan, risks, effort and hosting capacity |

Each criterion is scored out of five, with a threshold of three. The overall threshold is
70%; meeting thresholds does not guarantee funding.

## Length contract

Part B-1 sections 1–3 share an official **10-page limit**, including references, tables
and figures. **Benchmark adaptation:** 500 words per page yields **5,000 words**
for the whole submitted Part B-1, including headings, references, tables, captions and
in-text citations. There is no percentage tolerance. Excess words are disregarded.
Do not add a cover page, table of contents or appendix to extend the allowance. The
500-word estimate is a benchmark convention, not an official MSCA word-count rule.
