# Selected publications from the service-language research line

**Verified list, 24 September 2026**

The five published articles below are selected from Maite Sorell Valdeza's wider publication record. Titles, author order and identifiers follow the final published records. Contribution notes record the division of work within this research line.

## Published articles

**Sorell Valdeza, M. (2016). Repair sequences in municipal information encounters: an interactional account. Journal of Institutional Language Practices.**

DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-rural-service-language-team.2016.1

This article examines how residents and staff identify and repair misunderstandings during municipal information encounters. It draws on the author's doctoral and postdoctoral research. Its contribution is an analytic distinction between identifying an unfamiliar expression and resolving uncertainty about the next administrative action. Sorell Valdeza conducted the analysis and prepared the manuscript. The article established the interactional categories subsequently used in the team's work on service instructions.

**Sorell Valdeza, M., and Brizuela Laredo, T. (2019). When an appointment letter becomes an administrative barrier: comprehension and procedural obligations. Review of Language and Administrative Practice.**

DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-rural-service-language-team.2019.2

This article reports the internally supported study led by Sorell Valdeza. It combines linguistic analysis of appointment letters with legal analysis of the obligations represented in them. The analysis distinguishes interpreting an instruction from satisfying its procedural requirements. Sorell Valdeza led the empirical work; Brizuela Laredo examined relationships between wording, procedural duties and information rights.

**Sorell Valdeza, M., Nereda Soláns, A., and Lascuén Aroza, B. (2023). Separating comprehension from service access in rural care communication research. Studies in Rural Service Encounters.**

DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-rural-service-language-team.2023.3

This methodological article establishes the predecessor project's measurement distinctions. Published before the 2025 field study, it develops a framework for distinguishing comprehension, practical completion of administrative steps and receipt of care. Nereda Soláns contributed nursing interpretation of care pathways. Lascuén Aroza contributed the public-service research framework as overall project coordinator.

**Sorell Valdeza, M., Nereda Soláns, A., and Valtuerña Saz, E. (2026). Repeated language-access assessments among older rural service users: an observational study. Journal of Public Service Comprehension.**

DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-rural-service-language-team.2026.4

Published in March 2026, this article reports the paired assessment analysis summarised in the final project report, including the baseline comparison of completers and non-completers and the assessment reliability exercise. Sorell Valdeza led the analysis and manuscript; Nereda Soláns reviewed interpretation of care-related scenarios; Valtuerña Saz contributed supervised data checking and documentation of coding decisions.

**Sorell Valdeza, M., Brizuela Laredo, T., and Cendal Orduña, R. (2026). Language, paperwork and travel in older residents' accounts of rural care services. Review of Territorial Service Research.**

DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-rural-service-language-team.2026.5

Published in June 2026, this article integrates interview coding with analysis of administrative sequences using the predecessor project's interview sample. Brizuela Laredo analysed procedural obligations. Cendal Orduña mapped local service responsibilities and reviewed the interpretation of institutional procedures. The article examines how linguistic, administrative and territorial difficulties intersect within residents' accounts.
