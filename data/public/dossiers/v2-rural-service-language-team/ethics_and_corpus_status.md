# Interview corpus and access protocol

**University research governance memorandum, 24 September 2026**

Custodian: Dr Maite Sorell Valdeza, Universidad de Valdencina. This memorandum records the permissions attached to the completed rural service-language study, the materials retained and the institutional procedure for site access and participant enrolment.

## Existing permissions and materials

The university ethics committee approved the predecessor study under reference UV-CEI-2024-041 on 12 December 2024. Written site permissions covered the 15 participating service centres. Each of the 90 interview participants gave documented informed consent before enrolment, including consent to the interview, its recording, the assessments and the specified research use of the material.

The approved information sheet and consent process covered subsequent analysis of the interview corpus concerning language, administrative and territorial barriers in care-related service encounters. Secondary analysis within this scope is covered by UV-CEI-2024-041 and requires no further approval. This authorisation excludes new interviews, recontact for a different study, linkage to clinical records and unrestricted changes of research purpose.

The restricted corpus contains 90 interview transcripts and their associated audio, initial assessment records for all 90 participants and repeat assessment records for 72 participants. There are 162 assessment records in total. The 18 participants without repeat assessments remain represented by their initial assessments and interviews. Analytic files contain pseudonymous centre codes and research identifiers. Names and contact details are held separately. Access to the linkage key is limited to the designated custodian and authorised governance staff.

Some interviews contain incidental references to cultural or associational activities. The consent documents cover the approved service-encounter research purpose. They do not provide consent for a separate cultural-participation study. A material change of purpose requires a documented governance assessment and, where applicable, an ethics amendment or additional consent. The incidental references were collected without a common participation instrument and cannot constitute a validated participation measure.

## Site and consent registers

At 24 September 2026, the permission register contains entries only for the 15 centres under UV-CEI-2024-041. The consent register contains only the records of the 90 participants in that study. No new-site application or ethics amendment is on file. Existing site permissions apply to the named centres and the approved study.

## Procedure for new sites and participants

The institutional protocol prohibits recruitment into a new-centre study before both written permission from that centre and the person's documented informed consent are in place. Written permission must identify the authorised activity and the responsible site contact. Informal introductions and expressions of interest are recorded as correspondence, separately from the permission register.

Following written site permission and the applicable ethics approval, authorised site staff may circulate an approved information sheet. This information stage precedes enrolment. Researchers must provide an accessible explanation, establish that participation is voluntary and document consent before the person is recruited into the research sample or any research interview or assessment begins. Access to services must not depend on participation.

The protocol requires communication support appropriate to the person and a check that the explanation has been understood. Older age alone is not evidence of impaired decision-making capacity. Inclusion through proxy consent requires separate approved arrangements; the existing procedure covers participants providing their own consent.

## Storage, disclosure and analysis

Audio, transcripts and assessment files remain restricted. Pseudonymisation does not make them anonymous: combinations of village, service encounter and family circumstances can identify someone locally. Analysts remove unnecessary identifiers and review distinctive contextual details before using quotations. The custodian maintains the access register and records the purpose of each approved analyst's access.

The approved retention schedule permits restricted storage through 31 December 2030, followed by documented deletion or a separately authorised retention decision. Original consent records and governance documentation follow the university's applicable records schedule. Participants received a contact route for questions and requests concerning their data.

Publicly shareable materials comprise blank instruments, the codebook and disclosure-checked aggregate results. Individual-level files remain restricted. External collaborators receive access only after approval of their role, purpose and confidentiality obligations. Shared outputs are checked for small-cell disclosure and identifying combinations of contextual details before release.
