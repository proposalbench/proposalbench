# Maite Sorell Valdeza

Department of Language and Public Institutions, Universidad de Valdencina, Zaragoza, Spain.

## Appointment and qualifications

I hold a permanent appointment as Profesora Titular de Universidad in applied linguistics at Universidad de Valdencina, a Spanish public university. The appointment began on 1 September 2018 and has no contractual end date. I obtained national accreditation for this category in 2017.

My qualifications, all awarded by Universidad de Valdencina, are a degree in Spanish Philology, completed in 2006; a master's degree in discourse research, completed in 2008; and a PhD in Applied Linguistics, awarded in 2012. My doctoral dissertation examined comprehension and repair in municipal service encounters.

Following my doctorate, I held a university postdoctoral research appointment from 2012 to 2015 and a fixed-term lecturer appointment from 2015 to 2018. My current responsibilities combine undergraduate teaching, research, postgraduate supervision and departmental service. My allocated teaching for the 2025–2026 academic year was 160 contact hours, covering discourse analysis, institutional communication and research methods. I have two recognised six-year research periods, covering 2012–2017 and 2018–2023; the second was recognised in 2024.

## Research experience and funded responsibilities

My research examines how people understand service information, formulate requests and recover from misunderstandings in public institutions. I combine interactional analysis, interviews, document analysis and repeated measures of comprehension. Language access is examined through the relationships between users, documents, staff practices and institutional rules.

From 2017 to 2019, I was principal investigator of *Understanding municipal appointment letters*, supported by the Universidad de Valdencina internal research programme. I was responsible for its research design, ethics submission, analysis and final report. The study involved collaboration with municipal staff on the wording of appointment instructions and the procedural obligations those instructions conveyed.

From 1 January 2023 to 31 December 2025, I led the language-assessment work package in *Palabras de ventanilla y cuidados rurales*, funded by the regional Programa Aragonés de Investigación sobre Servicios de Proximidad, award PAISP-2022-017. I developed the assessment rubric, coordinated interviewer training, supervised paired-score analysis and led preparation of the language findings. Dr Berta Lascuén Aroza, Instituto de Estudios Cívicos de Valdemora, was overall scientific coordinator and was responsible for the consolidated project report.

## Publications, supervision and methods

My full publication record comprises 14 peer-reviewed articles and 5 book chapters. The accompanying selected publications list contains five articles from the service-language research line. Other outputs concern interactional repair, institutional discourse and methods of analysing public-service documents.

I currently supervise Elena Valtuerña Saz, enrolled as a doctoral researcher since October 2023. Her dissertation examines repair sequences in rural service interviews. Her work includes supervised coding, transcript checking and documentation of analytic decisions; her doctorate remains in progress.

My completed empirical work comprises observational studies, qualitative analyses and instrument development. I have experience coordinating interview consent procedures with older adults and integrating language findings with nursing and administrative-law interpretation.
