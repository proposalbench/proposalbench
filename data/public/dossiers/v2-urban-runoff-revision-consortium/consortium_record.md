# Consortium and consultation record

**Institutional record closed 23 September 2026**

The collaboration comprises four independent legal entities established in Spain, Belgium, Portugal and Italy. Each has its own governing body, accounts and employment contracts. There is no parent–subsidiary relationship, shared controlling shareholder or common institutional budget. The collaboration agreement governing the completed Runoff–Reception Ledger project remains the basis for exchanging permitted research outputs.

## Institutional responsibilities

**Universidad de Valdebruma del Litoral, Spain.** The coordinating university is based in Valencia. Elena Sorlí Valderra leads its urban planning and public-administration contribution. The university maintains the research record, administers interview consent and conducts spatial interpretation of drainage and exposure. Its institutional research office administered the earlier university award. Its ethics committee reviews research conducted under the university's responsibility.

**Institut indépendant d’hydrologie de Rivenne, Belgium.** This independent nonprofit research institute is based in Namur. Its lead investigator, Bastien Delvaux Orsanne, holds a doctorate in environmental engineering awarded in 2011. The institute develops rainfall–runoff models, maintains the computational event catalogue and checks separation between model fitting and evaluation. Its completed work includes event-based discharge estimation, instrument-quality assessment and uncertainty analysis.

**Instituto Costeiro da Enseada Clara, Portugal.** This independent nonprofit coastal research institute is based in Porto. Madalena Seabra Valmouro, whose doctorate in coastal engineering was awarded in 2010, leads receiving-water measurements and transport interpretation. Its laboratory analyses suspended solids and conducts controlled turbidity calibrations. Its contribution to the completed project was an outlet-to-estuary screening analysis, including interpretation of tidal reversals and the limits of sparse sampling.

**Associazione dei Comuni delle Acque Civiche, Italy.** This nonprofit association of municipalities is based in Bologna. Giulia Nardelli Vescora, an employee since 2017, leads its administrative research contribution. The association convenes technical officers, compares municipal procedures and maintains training materials. Decisions on warning thresholds, public access and operational procurement remain with the competent municipal bodies.

## Liaison assignment and decision register

On 7 April 2026 the association assigned Nardelli Vescora as the existing staff liaison for municipal adoption discussions. Her assignment covers maintaining a responsibility register, collecting written comments from competent offices and recording decisions. The register has separate fields for technical contact, operational owner, approving authority and maintenance responsibility. The operational-owner and approving-authority fields remain unfilled for all three study catchments.

On 8 September 2026 she circulated a draft threshold-workshop agenda to the partner leads. It distinguishes a model probability threshold, an administrative escalation rule and a public communication decision. The agenda includes review of false alarms, missed events, staff availability and the wording of escalation notices.

At closure, the association was still seeking agreement on a meeting date. Numerical thresholds and operating procedures had not reached municipal endorsement. The liaison retains the correspondence and records the institutional source of each proposed decision rule.

## Resident organisations

The university signed consultation agreements with Asociación Vecinal del Cauce de Llumera on 12 May 2026 and Associação de Moradores da Margem de Salgueiral on 2 June 2026. Both are independent adult resident organisations, respectively based in Spain and Portugal. Their representatives work with residents in low-lying rented housing and households facing language or mobility barriers.

The agreements cover organisational consultation on accessibility, meeting arrangements and information-sheet wording. Individual participation requires a separate invitation and consent process. Membership lists remain with the organisations. Attendance and reimbursement arrangements are subject to agreement for each activity.

No consultation sessions have yet been held, and individual recruitment has not opened. The Bologna-area community organisation approached by the association has not signed an agreement. The university holds the signed Spanish and Portuguese originals, and each organisation has received its countersigned copy.
