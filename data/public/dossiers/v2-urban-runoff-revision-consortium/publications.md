# Joint publications

**Bibliographic record verified internally on 18 September 2026**

The collaboration has four peer-reviewed articles listed below. All are published. Author names follow the partners' institutional records. The articles cover the successive methodological, environmental and administrative components of Runoff–Reception Ledger.

1. Sorlí Valderra, E.; Delvaux Orsanne, B.; Nardelli Vescora, G. (2023). **Recording drainage responsibility alongside storm observations: a three-country field framework.** *Journal of Urban Water Institutions*, 6(2), 41–58. DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-urban-runoff-revision-consortium.2023.1. This article describes the event-record structure and documentary coding approach used during fieldwork. Its supplementary dictionary links environmental observations to document references, responsible offices and the dates on which administrative information was obtained.

2. Delvaux Orsanne, B.; Sorlí Valderra, E.; Seabra Valmouro, M. (2024). **Event grouping and fitted runoff error in small coastal urban catchments.** *Methods in Drainage Observation*, 9(1), 15–33. DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-urban-runoff-revision-consortium.2024.2. The paper concerns model fitting in the interim field collection. It examines the effect of grouping residuals by event when estimating uncertainty. The final scientific report contains the completed collection and its corresponding fit statistics.

3. Seabra Valmouro, M.; Delvaux Orsanne, B.; Sorlí Valderra, E. (2025). **Interpreting suspended-material pulses between urban outlets and estuarine stations.** *Estuarine Field Systems Quarterly*, 4(3), 102–121. DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-urban-runoff-revision-consortium.2025.3. This paper uses the completed observation set. It discusses pulse timing, tidal reversal and the interpretation of paired outlet and receiving-water samples. Laboratory suspended-solids measurements are its principal concentration endpoint.

4. Nardelli Vescora, G.; Sorlí Valderra, E. (2026). **From an information contact to an authorised decision: interviews on municipal water warnings.** *Review of Municipal Evidence and Practice*, 8(2), 67–86. DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-urban-runoff-revision-consortium.2026.4. Published on 17 June 2026, this article analyses the 24 employee interviews. It distinguishes the distribution of environmental information from the administrative authorisation of an action and describes the coding procedure used for that distinction.
