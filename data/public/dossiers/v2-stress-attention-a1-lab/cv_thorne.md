**A. Personal Statement**
My research program focuses on the intersection of daily psychological stress, cognitive capacity (specifically attention), and health behaviors in adult outpatient populations. I have extensive experience designing and implementing Ecological Momentary Assessment (EMA) protocols to capture real-time fluctuations in stress and symptom severity. As a clinical psychologist, I am particularly interested in how transient cognitive deficits, such as reduced attentional control during periods of high stress, compromise patients' ability to adhere to complex medication routines. My laboratory at Oakhaven State University has successfully conducted several observational studies utilizing mobile health (mHealth) technologies to monitor behavioral routines. In the proposed resubmission, I will lead the research team, oversee the EMA and cognitive testing protocols, and direct the statistical analyses. I have a long-standing collaboration with Clara M. Jenkins-Brackendale, RN, MSN, which ensures our protocols are clinically grounded and feasible within the outpatient workflow.

**C. Contributions to Science**
1. Ecological Momentary Assessment of Stress in Clinical Populations. Early in my career, I focused on validating EMA protocols for capturing daily stress fluctuations in adults with chronic conditions. We demonstrated that high-frequency sampling (e.g., 4-6 times daily) is feasible and provides a more accurate representation of stress burden than retrospective questionnaires.
2. Mobile Cognitive Testing. I have contributed to the adaptation of traditional neuropsychological paradigms for mobile platforms. Specifically, my lab has worked on deploying Continuous Performance Tests (CPTs) on smartphones to measure sustained attention in naturalistic settings.
3. Behavioral Determinants of Medication Adherence. My recent work investigates the psychological barriers to medication adherence. We have identified that self-reported stress is a significant predictor of missed doses, independent of socioeconomic factors.
