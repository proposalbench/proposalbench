# Final Report: Echoes of the Agora (2024–2025)
**Date: 30 September 2026**

**Project Title:** Echoes of the Agora: Migrant Voices in Shared Cultural Spaces
**Funder:** Foundation for Mediterranean Civic Arts (Grant FMCA-2023-091)
**Duration:** 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2025
**Implementing Agency:** Associazione Voci del Patrimonio (AVP)

## 1. Executive Summary

The "Echoes of the Agora" project utilized shared cultural spaces in Palermo to engage residents facing severe language and migration-related barriers. By employing musical heritage sharing as an initial facilitation method, the project successfully transitioned informal community gatherings into structured listening workshops. The primary objective was to document the lived experiences of excluded residents regarding their access to local public services and to present these findings to municipal authorities.

## 2. Methodology and Engagement

The project's core activity consisted of a series of completed 2025 listening workshops held at the Fucina Civica Mediterranea, a community cultural space in Palermo. The methodology was strictly qualitative.

*   **Recruitment and Facilitation:** Participants were recruited via snowball sampling through local community networks. Workshops began with participatory interpretation of local and migrant musical heritage. Facilitators qualitatively observed that this non-verbal approach helped establish initial rapport with residents who typically avoid formal civic assemblies due to precarious migration statuses or language deficits.
*   **Qualitative Interviews:** Following the cultural facilitation phase, trained community facilitators conducted in-depth, semi-structured interviews. In total, the completed 2025 listening workshops produced 48 interviews with 48 distinct interviewees from 14 different countries of origin. Interviews were conducted in Arabic, French, and Italian.
*   **Transcription and Coding:** Bilingual facilitators translated and verified the transcripts. The 48 interviews were then subjected to rigorous qualitative coding using grounded theory principles to identify recurring barriers to public-service access, specifically in the domains of healthcare, municipal housing, and language support services.

## 3. Results and Municipal Uptake

The qualitative analysis of the 48 interviews generated a structured set of actionable demands. The project produced 120 coded recommendations, which were compiled into a comprehensive civic dossier presented to the Directorate for Civic Integration and other relevant municipal departments in October 2025.

The 120 coded recommendations were distributed across four primary sectors:
*   Healthcare Access and Navigability: 42 recommendations
*   Municipal Housing and Tenant Rights: 38 recommendations
*   Language and Translation Services: 25 recommendations
*   Cultural Space Utilization: 15 recommendations

To measure the project's impact on democratic decision-making, AVP tracked the formal responses from local authorities. By 30 September 2026, 18 of the 120 recommendations (15%) had received a written municipal response acknowledging the issue or committing to future review. Thus, uptake is 18 of 120 (15%), not 100%. No verified implementation of these commitments has been documented to date.

## 4. Data Management and Ethics

The project generated a substantial audio archive of musical sharing and spoken deliberation. Strict ethical protocols govern the use of this data to protect participants, many of whom face migration-status vulnerabilities. 

As of 30 September 2026, the audio archive's contributor agreement permits internal noncommercial analysis but forbids uploading recordings to third-party transcription services; local transcription is permitted and an existing offline workstation is available. The restriction applies equally to consented recordings. Public outputs may use authorised aggregate themes. All qualitative coding for the 120 recommendations was performed locally in compliance with these stipulations.

## 5. Update (September 2026): Financial Sustainability

To sustain the momentum of the 2025 listening workshops and adapt the facilitation method for more formal service-access deliberation, AVP has sought follow-on funding for the 2027 fiscal year. A EUR 40,000 municipal application remains pending as of 30 September 2026, with no award letter. This application, submitted in July 2026 to the Palermo Civic Initiatives Fund, aims to cover the operational costs of the Fucina Civica Mediterranea and the salaries of the community facilitators.
