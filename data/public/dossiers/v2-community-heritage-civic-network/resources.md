# Infrastructure and Resources
**Date: 30 September 2026**

The Civic Heritage Network consortium possesses specialized infrastructure across its three national nodes to support qualitative engagement, cultural facilitation, and secure data management.

## 1. Shared Cultural Spaces

**Fucina Civica Mediterranea (Palermo, Italy)**
Managed in partnership with AVP, this 300-square-meter community cultural space serves as the primary venue for the consortium's listening workshops and service-access deliberations. The space is fully accessible and equipped with modular seating to transition seamlessly from musical heritage sharing sessions to formal civic assemblies. It includes a soundproofed annex used for conducting confidential qualitative interviews with residents facing migration-related barriers.

**Kentro Fones (Athens, Greece)**
Operated by Koinonia Politismos (KP), this cultural center in the Kypseli neighborhood is optimized for participatory interpretation of local heritage. It features a community instrument library and a central hall designed for acoustic performances and subsequent community feedback sessions.

**Espacio Ágora (Valencia, Spain)**
Maintained by Red de Espacios Cívicos (REC), this facility is specifically designed to host dialogues between excluded residents and local authorities. It includes translation booths to support multilingual assemblies and a dedicated public administration liaison office.

## 2. Data Management and Transcription Infrastructure

Due to the sensitive nature of the qualitative data collected from vulnerable populations, the consortium maintains strict data sovereignty protocols. 

**Secure Audio Archive**
AVP hosts the consortium's central audio archive on an encrypted, air-gapped local server in Palermo. This archive contains the raw recordings of the 2025 listening workshops and musical sharing sessions.

**Offline Transcription Workstation**
To comply with our strict ethical guidelines, all qualitative data processing is conducted in-house. As of 30 September 2026, the audio archive's contributor agreement permits internal noncommercial analysis but forbids uploading recordings to third-party transcription services; local transcription is permitted and an existing offline workstation is available. The restriction applies equally to consented recordings. 

The existing offline workstation is a dedicated, air-gapped Dell Precision 3650 Tower equipped with 64GB of RAM. This hardware allows our community facilitators to generate transcripts that are subsequently verified by human bilingual checkers, without transmitting any audio data over the internet. Public outputs may use authorised aggregate themes derived from these locally processed transcripts.

## 3. Financial Resources

The consortium operates on a mix of philanthropic grants and municipal support. Currently, AVP's core operations are supported by EUR 12,000 in residual funds from the Foundation for Mediterranean Civic Arts, permitted for general administrative use until 31 December 2026. 

To support future activities at the Fucina Civica Mediterranea for the 2027 fiscal year, a EUR 40,000 municipal application remains pending as of 30 September 2026, with no award letter. Contingency arrangements for 2027 employment coverage are currently unresourced proposals under development; retention of the three experienced community facilitators is not secured beyond December 2026.
