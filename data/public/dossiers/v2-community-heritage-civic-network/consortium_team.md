# Consortium Profile: Civic Heritage Network
**Date: 30 September 2026**

## 1. Consortium Structure and Governance

The Civic Heritage Network is a transnational consortium dedicated to using cultural heritage and music as facilitation tools for democratic deliberation and public-service access. The consortium operates under a formal signed collaboration agreement executed on 12 May 2026. The partnership consists of three independent nonprofit civil-society bodies located in three EU Member States:

*   **Coordinator:** Associazione Voci del Patrimonio (AVP), based in Palermo, Italy. AVP is a registered nonprofit civil-society organization founded in 2015, specializing in migration sociology and the participatory interpretation of local heritage. AVP leads the consortium's methodological design and overall project management.
*   **Beneficiary 1:** Koinonia Politismos (KP), based in Athens, Greece. KP is an independent nonprofit organization established in 2017, focusing on the intersection of music, cultural spaces, and civic engagement for marginalized communities.
*   **Beneficiary 2:** Red de Espacios Cívicos (REC), based in Valencia, Spain. REC is an independent nonprofit civil-society body founded in 2016, dedicated to improving public administration responsiveness through community-led dialogue in shared cultural spaces.

The consortium's governance structure includes a Steering Committee comprising the directors of the three organizations, meeting quarterly to ensure strategic alignment, and a Methodological Board responsible for standardizing qualitative engagement practices across the three national contexts.
