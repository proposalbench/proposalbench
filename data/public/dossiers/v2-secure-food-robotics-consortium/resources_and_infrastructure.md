# Consortium Resources and Infrastructure

This document details the physical infrastructure, hardware, and laboratory facilities available to the consortium partners as of September 2026.

## 1. Robotics Hardware (Automazione Logistica Veloce S.r.l.)

ALV maintains a dedicated 800-square-meter mechatronics testing facility in Turin. The facility is equipped with motion capture systems for precise ground-truth tracking of robotic platforms. 

Regarding the robotic fleet available for research and development: A planned 30-device robot fleet has zero units acquired at present, as procurement is contingent on future capital expenditure approvals. However, six existing bench robots suffice for an initial bounded study. These bench robots are fully functional, scaled-down AGV prototypes equipped with the complete sensor suite (LiDAR, ultrasonic, RGB-D cameras) and compute modules intended for the final production models. They are ideal for validating navigation algorithms, fault-tolerance mechanisms, and edge-computing workloads in a controlled environment before scaling up to full-size industrial vehicles.

## 2. Food Microbiology Laboratory (Instituto de Microbiología Alimentaria de Aragón)

IMAA operates a state-of-the-art 400-square-meter BSL-2 laboratory in Zaragoza. The facility includes:

*   Four walk-in environmental chambers capable of simulating complex temperature and humidity profiles, essential for replicating interrupted cold chains.
*   High-throughput sequencing equipment for rapid microbial profiling.
*   Gas chromatography-mass spectrometry (GC-MS) for detecting volatile spoilage markers.

The lab can assay food samples to quantify microbial load, identify pathogens, and measure biochemical degradation over time. 

**Important Regulatory Note:** No medicinal-product development expertise or approval exists within IMAA or the broader consortium. The institute's mandate and certifications are strictly limited to food safety, agricultural research, and environmental monitoring. The team does not conduct pharmacological research, nor do they develop human or veterinary medicines.

## 3. Logistics Infrastructure (Alpen-Kühlkette eG)

AKK provides access to its primary distribution centre in Innsbruck, Austria. The facility spans 12,000 square meters and features three distinct temperature zones: ambient (15°C to 25°C), chilled (2°C to 8°C), and frozen (-25°C to -18°C). The warehouse is equipped with a dense network of IoT environmental sensors logging temperature and humidity at 5-minute intervals.

**Operational Update:**
Per an operator notice dated 21 September 2026, AKK management withdraws access to live refrigeration control interfaces for all external research projects due to updated internal risk management policies. Read-only monitoring remains authorised, allowing the consortium to continuously ingest real-time environmental data from the warehouse sensor network. Furthermore, an isolated thermal bench is available at the Innsbruck site; this standalone unit is physically decoupled from the main commercial storage areas and can be used for experimental manipulation of temperature profiles without risking commercial inventory.

Because live control of the commercial warehouse environment is no longer permitted, full validation of changed operating policies must be planned with access gates. Any proposed changes to AGV routing, door management, or cooling schedules generated by the AI decision-support system must pass through a human-in-the-loop verification step and be tested on the isolated thermal bench or via simulation before being considered for operational deployment.
