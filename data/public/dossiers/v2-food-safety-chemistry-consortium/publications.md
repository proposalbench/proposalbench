# Outputs of the IIMAV–Rzewska–Kelderspoor collaboration, 2023–2026

**Register closed:** 30 September 2026. Names are given in the publication order. This selection covers the collaboration's published journal articles during 2023–2026.

1. **Vauzernac, M.; Rouvessac, H.; Brzełowska, A.** (2023). 'Matrix-matched headspace calibration for chilled rice and poultry preparations.' *Journal of Quantitative Food Measurement*, 7(2), 41–56. DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-food-safety-chemistry-consortium.2023.1.

   This methods paper describes preparation of matrix-matched standards, blank correction and repeatability checks. Its scope is analytical measurement in prepared-food matrices. Vauzernac supervised the study; Rouvessac led the chemical measurements; Brzełowska reviewed the interpretation of the microbial reference work. The analytical procedures were subsequently used in the completed paired-lot study.

2. **Brzełowska, A.; Karszewicz, T.; Rouvessac, H.; Vauzernac, M.** (2024). 'Interpreting nonpathogenic surrogate growth during controlled refrigeration interruptions.' *Veterinary Food Process Studies*, 11(1), 15–29. DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-food-safety-chemistry-consortium.2024.1.

   The paper documents culture handling and the interpretation of surrogate growth in contained experiments conducted during 2023. It separates an experimental enumeration threshold from a regulatory criterion and explains why growth of the selected surrogate cannot establish the response of every foodborne pathogen.

3. **Velderschoot, S.; Drevenholt, L.; Vauzernac, M.** (2025). 'Replay and timestamp faults in laboratory cold-chain telemetry.' *Sensor Systems Security Methods*, 4(3), 88–103. DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-food-safety-chemistry-consortium.2025.1.

   This article presents the threat model and the 48-trace replay exercise reported in the final project report. Its contribution concerns gateway evidence and integrity rules. The evaluation uses archived laboratory traces, with attack construction and test conditions documented alongside the screening counts.

4. **Vauzernac, M.; Brzełowska, A.; Karszewicz, T.; Rouvessac, H.; Velderschoot, S.; Drevenholt, L.** (2026). 'Batch-held-out classification of surrogate growth from paired food measurements.' *Food Chain Measurement and Decisions*, 3(2), 61–79. DOI: 10.99999/pb.v2-food-safety-chemistry-consortium.2026.1.

   Published on 14 August 2026, this article reports the 144 complete pairs from Mésurafroid-25. The analysis uses the project's paired-lot archive and grouped validation procedure. The article describes the sensitivity interval and limitations arising from missing measurements and controlled exposure conditions.

The final project report and the restricted dataset are held in the project archive. Their custody and access conditions are recorded in the final report and the signed data-use schedule.
