# Mireille Vauzernac — curriculum vitae

**Position:** Head of Food Measurement and Process Evidence, Institut indépendant des mesures alimentaires de Valmorin (IIMAV), Nantes, France.

**Record updated:** 30 September 2026.

## Education and appointments

I received my doctorate in analytical chemistry from the Université des Sciences de Montreval in 2005. My dissertation examined matrix effects in headspace measurements of chilled prepared foods, using paired chemical measurements and culture-based spoilage indicators. My earlier training was in chemistry, with laboratory placements in chromatographic method development.

From 2005 to 2013 I worked as a research chemist at IIMAV's Nantes branch. My responsibilities included calibration studies, reference-material preparation and investigating disagreement between rapid measurements and microbiological results. From 2013 to 2019 I led the institute's chilled-food measurement group. Since 2019 I have headed Food Measurement and Process Evidence, with responsibility for laboratory quality, statistical review and supervision of the institute's contained food-process test line.

## Research responsibilities

My work concerns the interpretation of measurements when food temperature histories are incomplete or unreliable. I have supervised studies combining volatile profiles, temperature records and microbial counts, and have signed the corresponding analytical validation reports. I distinguish a model's ability to reproduce a laboratory endpoint from evidence that its use changes a food-chain outcome.

I was principal investigator of Mésurafroid-25, conducted from January 2023 through December 2025. I approved its sampling protocol, chaired the review of missing observations and authorised release of its paired-lot research dataset. The completed study used laboratory food lots and a nonpathogenic surrogate. Its endpoint was surrogate growth under controlled refrigeration conditions.

I currently coordinate the technical relationship between IIMAV, Rzewska Veterinary University in Poland and Kelderspoor Sensorbeveiliging BV in the Netherlands. This role includes maintaining the agreement register, resolving measurement terminology and checking laboratory documentation. Each partner retains responsibility for its own staff and facilities.

## Methods, outputs and supervision

My practical expertise includes headspace gas chromatography, matrix-matched calibration, repeatability assessment and the interpretation of microbial reference measurements. I review statistical analysis plans covering grouped validation, missingness and bootstrap uncertainty. Model implementation and security assessment are undertaken with specialist colleagues.

My career publication record comprises 41 peer-reviewed articles. The separate collaboration register lists four articles published during 2023–2026. These include our calibration method, interpretation of surrogate growth, telemetry replay experiments and batch-held-out classification results.

I have supervised eight completed doctorates: five as principal supervisor and three as co-supervisor. Three further doctoral researchers remain registered under my co-supervision. I have supervised six completed postdoctoral appointments.

My supervision has combined chemistry and veterinary microbiology, particularly the limits of surrogate organisms and the difference between an analytical alert and a food-release decision. I chaired IIMAV's analytical quality committee from 2016 to 2022 and have served on its research integrity committee since 2023. The quality committee maintained internal procedures for calibration traceability, duplicate measurements and reporting of detection limits.
