### Priority training needs

1. **Causal-inference and quasi-experimental methods:** I have no experience with instrumental variables, regression discontinuity designs, difference-in-differences with staggered adoption, or synthetic control methods. These techniques are increasingly important for evaluating health programmes using observational or quasi-experimental data, particularly when randomisation is not feasible at scale.

2. **Health economic evaluation:** I have not conducted cost-effectiveness or cost-benefit analyses. The NEVRAMAT trial did not include an economic component, which reviewers noted as a limitation.

3. **Advanced R programming for causal inference:** I would benefit from training in R packages such as `did`, `rdrobust`, `fixest`, and `CausalImpact`.

4. **Policy translation and stakeholder engagement at national level:** My engagement with health authorities has been at the regional level; I have limited experience with national-level policy processes.
