# Resources, infrastructure and partner organisations

As of September 2026.

## Current host institution

**Drelvani University (DU), Department of Global Health**
Maastricht, the Netherlands

The Department of Global Health at DU has 28 academic staff and 14 PhD candidates. It hosts the Maastricht Centre for Global Health Research (MCGHR), which coordinates collaborative projects in West and East Africa. Facilities include a secure data analysis environment (TSD-compliant), qualitative analysis lab with NVivo licences, and a REDCap server for electronic data capture. The department has an ongoing institutional collaboration agreement with the Neranvi Community Health Research Institute (NCHRI) in Ghana, renewed in 2023 for five years.

Dr. Owusu-Darko's current postdoctoral contract at DU runs until 31 August 2027.

**Supervisor at DU:** Prof. dr. Hendrel Vossenkade, Professor of Global Health Epidemiology. Expertise: maternal and child health programme evaluation, cluster-randomised trials in low-resource settings. H-index: 38.

## Partner organisation in Ghana (outgoing host)

**Neranvi Community Health Research Institute (NCHRI)**
Yeji, Bono East Region, Ghana

NCHRI is an independent research institute focused on community health in rural Ghana, with over 300 staff. It maintains a Health and Demographic Surveillance System (HDSS) covering approximately 156,000 residents in the Pru East and Sene West districts. NCHRI has laboratory facilities, a community engagement unit, a fleet of field vehicles, and a network of over 200 trained community health workers.

**Contact person and proposed outgoing supervisor:** Dr. Comfort Asante-Mensah, Senior Research Fellow, Maternal and Reproductive Health Unit, NCHRI. Dr. Asante-Mensah co-supervised Dr. Owusu-Darko's PhD and has expertise in community-based maternal health interventions, health systems research, and mixed-methods evaluation. She has confirmed in principle her willingness to host Dr. Owusu-Darko for a research stay at NCHRI.

## Partner organisation in Belgium

**Veltrenne Institute of Population Health (VIPH)**
Antwerp, Belgium

VIPH is a leading European institute for tropical medicine and global health, with approximately 400 researchers. The Department of Public Health has strong expertise in implementation science, health systems research, and causal-inference methods applied to programme evaluation in low- and middle-income countries.

**Contact person:** Prof. dr. Lieve Janssens-Willekens, Professor of Epidemiology and Causal Inference, Department of Public Health, VIPH. Prof. Janssens-Willekens leads the Health Impact Evaluation Group, which specialises in quasi-experimental and econometric methods (difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity, instrumental variables, synthetic control) for evaluating health programmes in sub-Saharan Africa. She has offered in principle to host Dr. Owusu-Darko for a research fellowship at VIPH.

Prof. Janssens-Willekens and Dr. Owusu-Darko met at the 13th European Congress on Tropical Medicine and International Health (ECTMIH) in Utrecht in November 2023 and have since discussed potential collaboration on applying causal-inference methods to the NEVRAMAT dataset and its scale-up.
