# Study team and clinical sites: Post-Discharge Routines Study

College of Nursing, Cuyahoga Ridge University. Status as of 30 September 2026.

**Dr. Okonkwo-Lattimore** (PhD, clinical psychology, 2011) directs the CRU Stress and Cognition Assessment Lab. She adapted the 3-minute mobile Psychomotor Vigilance Task (PVT) used in Phases 1 and 2, prepared the past-week PSS-10 wording used at the Phase 2 weekly visits, trained study staff in PSS-10 and PVT administration, and wrote the distress protocol under which coordinators refer participants who report high distress.

**Dr. Lindqvist** (PhD, biostatistics, 2013) works on sensitivity analysis for informative dropout in longitudinal studies. He was not part of the A0, which budgeted 3% effort from the College's shared biostatistics core without a named statistician. He joined after the summary statement and wrote the missing-data analysis plan dated 24 August 2026 (summarized in feasibility_report.md, section 4).

**Ms. Brandvold** consented all Phase 1 and Phase 2 participants, conducted the weekly visits and managed MEMS cap issue, return and download. Her position is funded by the College and does not depend on grant support.

No team member is a pharmacist or has worked with pharmacy dispensing or refill data.

## Clinical sites

### Ridgeline Transitional Care Clinic, Cuyahoga Ridge Health (CRH), Cleveland

Nurse-practitioner-led clinic that sees adults within 14 days of discharge from CRH medical units; about 210 new post-discharge patients per month in 2025 (clinic statistics). Site of Phase 1 and Phase 2. Letter of support signed 14 August 2026 by the medical director, Priya Raghunathan-Lowe, MD: an examination room for weekly visits on two weekdays, a referral workflow through clinic nurse practitioners, and support for recruitment of up to 60 participants over an 18-month enrollment period. An earlier letter of general support (22 January 2026) accompanied the A0.

### Lakevale Community Health Center, Lorain, Ohio

Federally qualified health center with a post-hospital follow-up clinic. The A0 named Lakevale as the source of 45 of the 90 participants, based on conversations with its medical director, Owen T. Marchbanks, DO; no letter was obtained before submission.
