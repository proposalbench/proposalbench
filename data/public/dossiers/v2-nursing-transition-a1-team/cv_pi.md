## Research focus

How adults manage medications in the first weeks after hospital discharge, and how daily stress and lapses of attention relate to the routines they keep. Methods: intensive longitudinal designs (ecological momentary assessment, daily diaries), structured weekly assessments, electronic event monitoring (since 2026) and semi-structured interviews.
