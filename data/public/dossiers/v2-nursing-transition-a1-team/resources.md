## Human subjects approvals and consent scope

| Protocol | Status |
|---|---|
| STUDY2024-0177 (Phase 1) | Approved 14 August 2024; closed 15 December 2025 |
| STUDY2026-0118 (Phase 2) | Approved 2 April 2026 (expedited, minimal risk); enrollment closed 3 July 2026; open for data analysis |

The Phase 2 consent form and HIPAA authorization cover: a MEMS Cap on one index medication; daily medication and stress diaries; weekly visits with PSS-10 and PVT; and review of the CRH discharge summary to record the discharge medication list. They do not cover access to pharmacy dispensing or refill records, linkage of study data to pharmacy records, or recontact for that purpose.

No amendment requesting pharmacy-record linkage has been submitted to or approved by the CRU IRB. No data use agreement exists with the CRH outpatient pharmacy or any external pharmacy or claims source. Participants in Phases 1 and 2 were not asked to consent to future linkage.
