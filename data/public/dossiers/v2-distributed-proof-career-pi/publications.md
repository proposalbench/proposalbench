## Under Review

1.  **Thorne, E. V.**, & Quenneville, S. L. (2026). "Spectral Bounds for Sensor-Fault Inference in Observability Graphs." *Oakhaven Spectral Systems Studies*. Submitted July 2026. **[Status as of 25 September 2026: Under Review]**
    *Abstract:* We investigate the theoretical limits of fault inference in distributed sensor networks modeled as discrete-time linear systems without noise. Assuming an unknown but fixed set of at most $s$ compromised sensor channels over an observation horizon $T$, we analyze the $T$-step observability operator. We establish preliminary bounds showing that if the system satisfies $2s$-sparse observability, the initial state and the non-zero injected faults can be uniquely recovered. We demonstrate that maintaining system-wide state estimation follows as a corollary of this successful fault isolation. Validation is proposed via simulated autonomous agents.
    *DOI:* 10.99999/pb.v2-distributed-proof-career-pi.2026.1

## Published / Accepted

2.  **Thorne, E. V.** (2025). "Convergence Guarantees for Bounded-Delay Consensus on Fixed Connected Graphs." *Journal of Theoretical Distributed Systems*, 14(3), 210-228.
    *Abstract:* This paper proves that continuous-time consensus algorithms converge over fixed, undirected topologies despite uniform communication delays. We establish that for a symmetric delayed Laplacian system with coupling rate $\kappa$ and maximum eigenvalue $\lambda_{max}$, asymptotic consensus is guaranteed for any continuous initial history if $\kappa \tau \lambda_{max} < \pi/2$. For networks with $\lambda_{max} \le 10$ and $\kappa = 0.1$ s$^{-1}$, this yields a delay margin of $\tau \le 1.5$ seconds.
    *DOI:* 10.99999/pb.v2-distributed-proof-career-pi.2025.1

3.  **Thorne, E. V.**, & Quenneville, H. R. (2024). "Algebraic Connectivity and Information Flow in Time-Varying Networks." *Annals of Network Security*, 9(1), 45-62.
    *Abstract:* We analyze the rate of information diffusion in networks where edges appear and disappear according to a Markov process. We provide a lower bound on the expected time to consensus based on the time-averaged algebraic connectivity of the network.
    *DOI:* 10.99999/pb.v2-distributed-proof-career-pi.2024.1

4.  **Thorne, E. V.**, & Sterling, J. C. (2022). "Eigenvalue Perturbation in Dynamic Laplacian Matrices." *Journal of Applied Matrix Dynamics*, 41(2), 112-119.
    *Abstract:* A summary of the core findings from the first author's doctoral dissertation, detailing how small perturbations in edge weights affect the Fiedler value (second smallest eigenvalue) of graph Laplacians.
    *DOI:* 10.99999/pb.v2-distributed-proof-career-pi.2022.1

5.  Sterling, J. C., & **Thorne, E. V.** (2021). "A Note on the Spectral Radius of Bipartite Consensus Graphs." *Meridian Network Mathematics Review*, 7(4), 301-315.
    *Abstract:* We provide a tight upper bound on the spectral radius for a specific class of bipartite graphs used in distributed averaging protocols.
