## Data holdings and governance

The final archive contains records for 300 baseline dwellings and 276 follow-up dwellings, with participant-level linkage maintained separately. Raw sensor files, quality flags, questionnaire records and renovation descriptions are versioned. Reproducible scripts generate the reported analysis populations and document exclusions caused by missing measurements.

The public archive contains metadata, blank instruments, the data dictionary and analysis code. It contains no household-level health records or precise dwelling coordinates. Access to de-identified individual-level data requires a scientific review, an appropriate legal basis and a data-use agreement. Landlords cannot obtain individual symptom responses through that process. The published tables use disclosure controls for small groups.
