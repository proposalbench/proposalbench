# Institutional Resources and Agreements

**Date:** 30 September 2026
**Prepared by:** Radim Hlubocký and Prof. Dr. Milena Vranokvětová

## 1. Host Institution Support

The University of Central Bohemia (UCB) provides full institutional support for this doctoral project. 
- **Faculty Sponsor:** Prof. Dr. Milena Vranokvětová is the existing employed faculty sponsor and assumes full supervisory responsibility for all grant applications led by Radim Hlubocký.
- **Host Support Letter:** A formal letter of host support has been signed by the Dean of the Faculty of Science, confirming the provision of office space, administrative backing, and basic infrastructure through June 2028.

## 2. Departmental Infrastructure

The Department of Theoretical Astrophysics provides access to a dedicated high-performance workstation for local processing.
- **Workstation Specs:** 64-core AMD Threadripper, 256 GB RAM, 8 TB NVMe storage.
- **Availability:** 100% dedicated to the sparse-source recovery project.

## 3. Observatory Data Agreements

Observational data for the planned tests will be sourced from the Moravian Stellar Observatory (MSO). 

**MSO Data Access Agreement (Signed 12 May 2025):**
- The agreement grants Radim Hlubocký and Prof. Vranokvětová access to the MSO 1.5m telescope raw data archive and the raw image frames of the digitised historical logbooks.
- The existing observatory agreement strictly forbids uploading raw frames (including telescope images and logbook scans) to any external cloud services (including commercial cloud providers and external academic computing grids). 
- All processing of raw frames must be conducted locally on secured UCB departmental infrastructure.
- Public simulated frames and derived numerical catalogs (once stripped of raw sensor data) are allowed to be uploaded to external services or repositories.
- **Status:** No cloud waiver exists as of 30 September 2026, and no application for a waiver is currently pending.

## 4. Archival Records Access

For the stretch goal involving historical observing logs and selection bias, we require access to the MSO physical and digitised logbooks from 1998 to 2010.
- **Status:** A formal request for these records was submitted to the MSO archive custodian on 10 August 2026. The archive custodian has not yet supplied the expected access turnaround time for retrieving and digitising these specific logs.

## 5. Host Funding-Request Guidance

All external funding requests submitted through UCB must adhere to the internal grant office guidelines (Updated January 2026):
- **Indirect Costs:** UCB mandates a flat 20% indirect cost rate on all direct costs, unless explicitly capped by the funder.
- **Equipment:** Purchases of major equipment (defined as single items > EUR 10,000) require a separate departmental justification.
- **Compute Services:** Any budget requesting external compute services (e.g., for processing the permitted public simulated frames) must include a formal quotation from the provider attached to the internal routing form. (Note: The required quotation for external compute services has not yet been obtained).
