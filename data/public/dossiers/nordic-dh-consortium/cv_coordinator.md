## 3. Research Profile and Methodological Scope
My research focuses on the quantitative modelling, thermodynamic optimization, and operational control of large-scale thermal energy storage (TES) systems, particularly pit thermal energy storage (PTES) integrated into low-temperature district heating (LTDH) networks. We employ advanced energy systems modelling, hardware-in-the-loop simulations, and techno-economic optimization to maximize the utilization of waste heat and renewable energy sources.

**Methodological Boundaries:** Our group focuses strictly on the engineering, thermodynamic, and quantitative techno-economic modelling of energy systems. We do not conduct qualitative social science research, nor do we have expertise in energy justice, social fairness evaluations, or the design of fair consumer tariffs. Furthermore, while we monitor indoor climate parameters (temperature, overheating, air quality) to evaluate building energy performance, we have no clinical partnerships, ethical approvals, or methodological expertise to measure health outcomes in occupants.
