# Facilities, holdings and permissions statement

Rannakaar University, School of Cultural Evidence

Inventory and rights status: 18 September 2026

## Existing facilities

The team has two university-owned desktop workstations, each with 32 GB RAM and a 1 TB solid-state drive. Both are operational and available through the school's ordinary research facilities allocation. The installed workflow uses Python, NumPy, SciPy and an audio editor for inspection, parameter recording and digital denoising.

A managed university file service stores the authoritative project directory, including untouched originals, versioned metadata and processed derivatives. Routine institutional backups cover this directory. The desktops hold working copies. Access to participant-linked files is limited to named team members and authorised repository staff.

The university's shared digitisation room has a copy stand, a digital camera, diffuse lighting and a book cradle suitable for the loose leaves and supported manuscript openings used in the pilot. Collection staff supervise handling and maintain the link between source identifiers and image filenames. These facilities supplied the pilot images. Their continued availability under the ordinary internal booking system is confirmed in the host letter.

The school's existing recording room, audio interface and microphone supported the contemporary performance examples. The recording chain is sufficient to replay, inspect and process the existing files. The completed corpus can be analysed as deposited.

The November 2025 facilities review recorded a supplier quotation for an overhead scanner at EUR 28,000, including delivery and VAT. The quotation was not pursued and expired in February 2026; no order was placed and the scanner was not purchased. The pilot completed its digitisation through the existing shared room.

## Source and participant permissions

The Rannakaar Manuscript and Sound Collection is an internal university collection. The university holds the physical manuscripts used in the pilot and controls access to them under its collection procedures. Custody of an object is recorded separately from permission to publish its image.

The collection director signed the pilot reproduction and publication permission on 12 June 2025. It covers the selected source images, annotated excerpts, catalogue descriptions and publication of the described pilot results. It does not automatically cover other material in the collection. The signed document is held with the source-access register.

All eight performers signed participation and use agreements before recording. The agreements cover research processing, creation of denoised and synthetically masked derivatives, and publication of the designated audio examples and related pilot findings. They exclude unrestricted redistribution of the complete recordings and voice identification or voice cloning.

The university ethics panel issued its favourable determination on 22 November 2023, before recruitment began. The determination covers secondary research analysis of the recorded examples within the terms of the participant agreements, without time limit. Recruitment and recording ended during the completed pilot. No participant withdrawal was recorded by 18 September 2026. No new recruitment is active, and there is no pending ethics application for an extension of the study.
