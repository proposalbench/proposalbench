# Facilities, Resources, and Team

## Laboratory Facilities
The Adolescent Chronobiology and Development Lab (ACDL), directed by Dr. Marston Vancura, occupies 1,200 sq ft of dedicated research space in the Department of Psychology at the University of Oakhaven. The lab includes a reception area, two private assessment rooms for interviews and anthropometric measurements, a secure data processing room, and workspace for graduate students and postdoctoral fellows. 

**Equipment:**
The lab is heavily equipped for objective behavioral sleep measurement. We currently own and maintain 150 ActiGraph GT9X Link monitors and 50 Philips Respironics Actiwatch 2 devices. We possess the necessary initialization hubs, charging stations, and proprietary software (ActiLife for the ActiGraph devices; Actiware for the Actiwatch 2 devices) for high-throughput data processing.

**Equipment Gaps:**
The lab is strictly equipped for behavioral and actigraphic data collection. We do not have blood pressure cuffs, ambulatory blood pressure monitors, Holter monitors, or phlebotomy capabilities. We have no clinical trial infrastructure and no staff trained in clinical cardiovascular or metabolic assessments.

## Computing Resources
The lab houses 4 high-performance workstations dedicated to actigraphy data scoring and statistical modeling. Data are stored on the University of Oakhaven's secure, HIPAA-compliant server, which features automated nightly backups and multi-factor authentication access.

- **Calla Arislow, M.S. (Lab Manager):** Manages IRB protocols, school district relationships, device inventory, and undergraduate research assistants.
