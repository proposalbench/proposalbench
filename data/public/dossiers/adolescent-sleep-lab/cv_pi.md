## Research Focus
My research examines the developmental trajectories of sleep duration, timing, and regularity during puberty, and their associations with behavioral health and weight status in rural adolescent populations. All research conducted to date has been strictly observational and mechanistic. I have never conducted a clinical trial, and my laboratory does not maintain clinical trial infrastructure.
