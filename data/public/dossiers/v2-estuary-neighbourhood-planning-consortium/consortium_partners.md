# Delta Neighbourhood Water Consortium: partners

Status: September 2026

The consortium consists of three legal entities in three EU member states. None is owned or controlled by another, and they have no common board. The partners have worked together since January 2024 in PB-BUURTGETIJ-26 (see final report).

The consortium participation agreement was signed by ZUT on 18 June 2026, by WEIH on 25 June 2026 and by FBMI on 2 July 2026. It covers roles, background intellectual property, publication rules and the application of existing data agreements. It runs until 31 December 2029.

## ZUT (coordinator)

The Section Urban Water Planning studies drainage-led neighbourhood design and how the benefits of adaptation measures are distributed among households. Prof. Hoogvliet-Ramaker is the legal and scientific coordinator and signs for ZUT.

The doctoral candidate Mirthe Zwanenburg-Teeuw leads the spatial analysis: block-level exposure, distributional statistics and uncertainty estimation. She works under the supervision of Prof. Hoogvliet-Ramaker and Dr. ir. Koen Stamhuis-Weel and has no consortium management role.

ZUT runs the 1D–2D urban drainage model used in PB-BUURTGETIJ-26, owns the temporary logger network and hosts the household survey data. ZUT has no marine field capacity and no water-quality laboratory.

## WEIH

WEIH was founded in 1989 and has 52 staff, of whom 31 are scientists. Its Department of Estuarine and Catchment Hydrology is led by Dr. Bruhnkötter and covers:

- rainfall-runoff modelling in lowland catchments of the Ems and Weser basins;
- tidal hydrodynamics, salinity and suspended sediment in the Ems and Weser estuaries, using TELEMAC-2D and an in-house 1D network model.

WEIH operates 14 water-level and discharge gauges in Lower Saxony. It runs a research ensemble discharge forecast for two catchments; this forecast is not an operational warning service.

In PB-BUURTGETIJ-26, WEIH set up a 1D network model of the tidal rivers around the Island of Dordrecht and produced the 50-member ensemble of river-level boundary conditions. WEIH has not modelled the transport or decay of faecal bacteria or chemical contaminants.

Track record: two DFG research grants on estuarine sediment dynamics (2016–2019, 2020–2024).

## FBMI

FBMI was founded in 1996 and has 38 staff. Dr. Skovsende heads Field Oceanography. FBMI measures in the Danish Wadden Sea and along the North Sea coast:

- CTD and turbidity transects;
- currents with ADCP;
- surface flow with GPS drifter releases.

Between 2019 and 2024 FBMI collected 1,240 bathing-water samples at 18 beaches for *E. coli* and intestinal enterococci. Its ISO/IEC 17025 scope includes *E. coli* in surface waters by ISO 9308-3 (most probable number), intestinal enterococci by ISO 7899-2 (membrane filtration), and dissolved nutrients.

For six of these beaches, sampled once or twice a week in the 2019–2022 bathing seasons and two to three times a week in 2023, FBMI developed a logistic model that predicts exceedances from 48-hour rainfall, discharge at the nearest stream outlet, wind direction and tidal phase. An exceedance is a single sample above 500 MPN/100 mL *E. coli* or above 200 CFU/100 mL intestinal enterococci. These are study alarm thresholds that borrow the numbers of the 'good quality' class for coastal waters in Directive 2006/7/EC. The Directive assesses those numbers as 95th percentiles over a multi-season record, so an exceedance in this model is not a regulatory classification of the sample. The model was fitted to 486 samples from 2019–2022 (61 exceedances) and tested on the 2023 season at the same beaches: 192 samples, 34 exceedances and 158 non-exceedances. ROC AUC was 0.81 (95% CI 0.71 to 0.89). The interval comes from a bootstrap over sampling days (2,000 replicates) that kept all beaches sampled on the same day together. With six beaches, dependence between repeated samples at the same beach was not resampled separately, and the interval may be too narrow. The evaluation is documented in FBMI technical note FN-2024-03.

In PB-BUURTGETIJ-26, FBMI checked logger calibration and ran a floodwater sampling pilot (31 samples).

FBMI has no in-house assays for chemical contaminants such as PFAS or pharmaceutical residues. The FBMI board approved the purchase of an LC-MS/MS system on 12 May 2026, and delivery is expected in the second quarter of 2027. Method validation has not started and no measurements exist.

## Joint record

- PB-BUURTGETIJ-26 (2024–2026): shared fieldwork, ensemble modelling and three co-authored PB-BUURTGETIJ-26 articles.
- Partner meeting and model exchange workshop, Oldenburg, 18–19 November 2025.

The parts of a chain from catchment runoff through estuarine transport to coastal sites exist at separate partners, but the consortium has not yet coupled them in one study. No partner has built or operated a public early-warning service.
