## Current position

Full professor of Urban Water Planning at ZUT since 1 March 2017. Chair of the Section Urban Water Planning, which had 14 staff and 6 PhD candidates in September 2026.

Coordinator of the Delta Neighbourhood Water Consortium. The other members are the Weser-Ems Institut für Hydrologie e.V. and the Fanø Bay Marine Institute. Signed the consortium participation agreement on behalf of ZUT on 18 June 2026.
