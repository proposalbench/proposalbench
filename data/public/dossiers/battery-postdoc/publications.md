## Published journal articles

### 1. Pellet geometry and contact contributions in impedance measurements of lithium argyrodites

Nestravelli, D.; Vardessolvi, E.; Bellorvessi, M. (2021). *Selvaronda Journal of Inorganic Ion Transport*, 8, 117–129.

DOI: 10.99999/pb.battery-postdoc.2021.1

Status: published, 18 November 2021. Vardessolvi is second author. She prepared pellets, measured their dimensions and collected part of the impedance dataset during her doctorate. Nestravelli led the measurement comparison and manuscript drafting. Bellorvessi supervised the research. The article addresses measurement practice and does not report the later SULVERNEX cycling experiment.

### 2. Batch variability in mechanochemically prepared Li6PS5Cl solid electrolytes

Vardessolvi, E.; Nestravelli, D.; Bellorvessi, M. (2023). *Velnarion Materials Chemistry and Ionic Transport*, 12, 204–218.

DOI: 10.99999/pb.battery-postdoc.2023.1

Status: published, 7 September 2023. Vardessolvi is first author. She developed the sample-tracking scheme, performed synthesis and conductivity measurements, analysed the data and wrote the initial manuscript. This paper derives from her doctoral work. Its batches are separate from those prepared for SULVERNEX, and its measurements must not be pooled with the final project's dataset without accounting for the changed processing records.

### 3. Reproducible resistance extraction from pressure-controlled sulfide composite cells

Vardessolvi, E.; Bellorvessi, M.; Sörnelqvist, L. (2024). *Sörvaskel Electrochemical Measurement Methods*, 6, 51–66.

DOI: 10.99999/pb.battery-postdoc.2024.1

Status: published, 12 December 2024. Vardessolvi is first author. She performed the electrochemical measurements, implemented the fitting workflow and drafted the manuscript. Bellorvessi supervised the experimental work. Sörnelqvist advised on reproducibility and the interpretation of overlapping impedance features through remote collaboration. The article reports a measurement-validation dataset rather than the complete coating comparison. Its cells are not included in the 24-cell SULVERNEX endpoint analysis.
