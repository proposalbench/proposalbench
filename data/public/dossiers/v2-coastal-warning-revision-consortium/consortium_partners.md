# OUTFALERT consortium: partners, team and resources

Status at 30 September 2026

## Participation

Three legal entities in three countries have signed letters of commitment to take part in a joint Horizon Europe application continuing the OUTFALERT work: CICS (9 September 2026), URC (11 September 2026) and WISW (16 September 2026). A consortium memorandum of understanding covering roles, background intellectual property and data access was signed by all three on 18 September 2026.

| No. | Entity | Country | Legal status | Main expertise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cladach Institute for Coastal Science (CICS), Galway | Ireland | Independent research organisation; company limited by guarantee (2009); registered charity | Coastal water quality, hydrodynamic modelling, microbiology |
| 2 | Université de Rochefort-Charente (URC), Laboratoire de Chimie des Eaux Littorales (LCEL) | France | Public university | Environmental chemistry, organic-matter fluorescence |
| 3 | Weser-Institut für Stadt- und Wasserplanung (WISW) e.V., Bremen | Germany | Non-profit registered association | Urban water and stormwater planning |

The three entities have no shared ownership, control or management.

### CICS Shannon Estuary Laboratory

The Shannon Estuary Laboratory in Foynes, Co. Limerick, opened in March 2024, is a department of CICS. It has no separate legal personality, company registration or PIC; its contracts, accounts and legal signatory are those of CICS. Head: Dr Eoin Fennessy-Ward; 5 staff. In April–May 2026 it ran the proxy assay on 20 split samples from Galway; its exceedance classification agreed with the Galway laboratory in 18 of 20 samples. It has collected no samples from the Shannon estuary.

## Team and facilities

### CICS (52 staff; income EUR 4.3 million in 2025)

- Dr Sorcha Mulqueeney: coordinator; chemical oceanography (see CV).
- Dr Eoin Fennessy-Ward: head of the Shannon Estuary Laboratory; PhD in environmental microbiology (2015); developed the glucuronidase assay protocol.
- Dr Méabh Costigan-Reidy: hydrodynamic modeller; built the TELEMAC-2D model of inner Galway Bay.
- Dr Ruairí Tobin-Hayes: statistician; author of the outfalert-uq code; covers data management at 0.2 FTE. CICS has no dedicated data steward.
- Two laboratory technicians (Galway).

Facilities: microbiology laboratory accredited to ISO/IEC 17025 for ISO 9308-3; three field fluorometers; glucuronidase assay set-ups in Galway and Foynes; 7-m survey boat; 512-core compute cluster.

EU track record: work-package lead in ESTUWARD-PB19 (Interreg Atlantic Area 2014–2020; 2019–2022). CICS has not coordinated a Horizon 2020 or Horizon Europe project.

### URC, LCEL

- Prof. Clémence Arbogast-Riou: environmental chemistry; excitation–emission matrix (EEM) fluorescence and PARAFAC; host of Dr Mulqueeney's 2012–2014 fellowship.
- Dr Bastien Morvillier: environmental microbiology; runs culture methods.
- One engineer; one PhD student (thesis on organic-matter fluorescence in the Charente, started October 2025).

Facilities: EEM spectrofluorometer; culture laboratory; small boat at Rochefort. Culture methods run under the university quality system; the laboratory is not accredited for ISO 9308-3. EU track record: partner in two Horizon 2020 projects (2016–2020 and 2018–2022).

### WISW (31 staff)

- Dr.-Ing. Henrike Wolfsteller: urban water planning; overflow-reduction programmes and stormwater master plans.
- Jonas Kettler, M.Sc.: planning economics; drafted the decision-cost table (July 2026).
- Dr. Lene Brakhage: participatory planning; municipal workshops.

Experience: stormwater master plans and overflow-reduction studies for three municipalities in Lower Saxony and Bremen (2016–2024); partner in one Interreg North Sea Region project. WISW has no water-quality monitoring or laboratory capacity and had no experience with bathing-water warning systems before joining OUTFALERT in 2025.

## Users and municipalities

- The Galway local authority provided beach-attendance counter data to BAYPROXY (2022–2024), and its bathing-water officer attended two BAYPROXY meetings in 2023. It has not reviewed the decision-cost table and has not given a letter of support.
- WISW held exploratory calls with the drainage departments of two municipalities in Lower Saxony (12 May and 9 June 2026) about overflow notification for urban watercourses. Both asked to be kept informed; neither has committed staff, data or a letter.
- No municipality, water utility or bathing-water authority is a member of the consortium.
- The consortium holds no monitoring data from inland urban watercourses or river bathing sites; all its contamination data come from estuaries and coastal bathing waters.

## Collaboration history

CICS and URC have worked together since 2012 (fellowship; two joint publications). CICS and WISW have worked together since February 2025 (OUTFALERT preparation; decision-cost table 2026).
