# Team and Institutional Resources

*As of September 2026*

## Current Team

### PI: Rachel M. Ostrander, Ph.D.
Associate Professor (tenured), Department of Education Policy & Economics, Velmarden University. Expertise in causal inference for education interventions, cost-effectiveness analysis, and randomised field experiments. 12 years of experience conducting school-based RCTs. No background in computer science, artificial intelligence, or machine learning.

### Co-PI: Marcus J. Treviño, Ph.D.
Associate Professor, School of Teaching and Learning, Velmarden University. Expertise in middle-grades mathematics education, qualitative research methods, and tutor–student interaction analysis. Former middle-school mathematics teacher (3 years). No background in computer science, artificial intelligence, or machine learning.

### Research Staff

- **Dr. Yunara Pellen**, Postdoctoral Research Associate (appointed September 2024, funded through August 2027 by Velmarden University). Specialises in multilevel modelling and psychometrics. Supports data analysis and instrument development.
- **Two doctoral research assistants** (Department of Education Policy & Economics), available for project work on a half-time basis.

## District Partnership

Velmarden University has a standing Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Cedarquill Metropolitan School District (CQMSD) for collaborative research, renewed in 2023 and valid through 2028. CQMSD serves approximately 47,000 students across 78 schools; 81% qualify for free or reduced-price lunch; 49% Black, 29% Hispanic, 13% White, 9% other. The district has expressed interest in continuing the RatioWeave-Tutor programme and in exploring how technology might support tutor effectiveness, but no formal commitment for a new study has been made.

Key district contact: Dr. Alicia R. Veldren, Executive Director of Curriculum and Instruction, CQMSD.

## Institutional Resources

- **Velmarden Center for Education Research (VCER):** Provides IRB support, data management infrastructure, and secure servers for restricted-use education data. The center has no AI or computing research facilities.
- **Survey and Measurement Core:** Supports instrument development, cognitive interviewing, and psychometric analysis.
- **Office of Research Computing:** General-purpose high-performance computing cluster available to all faculty; the team has not used it for prior work.

## Gaps

The team does not include any member with expertise in computer science, artificial intelligence, machine learning, natural language processing, or educational technology development. All prior research has involved human-delivered instruction without adaptive software or AI-based tools. The team has no existing collaborations with computer science or engineering departments.

The team's statistical computing is conducted in Stata and R; no member has experience with Python-based machine learning frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, TensorFlow) or with developing or evaluating AI systems.
