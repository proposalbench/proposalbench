# GUICHETS consortium: partners, roles and agreements

Status at 30 September 2026.

## 1. Coordinator: Seuil Commun asbl (Belgium)

- Legal form: association sans but lucratif (asbl) under the Belgian Code of Companies and Associations; registered office in Liège; founded 2009.
- Nonprofit civil-society organisation. Recognised by the Wallonia Region as an initiative locale d'intégration since 2015. Income 2025: EUR 948,000.
- Activities: advice clinic on social rights and residence procedures (1,140 individual consultations in 2025, staffed by two jurists who do not facilitate deliberation sessions); interpreted information sessions; resident deliberation since 2024.
- Staff: 14 persons, 11.2 FTE.

| Person | Role | Background |
|---|---|---|
| Dr Annick Vercauteren-Delhez | Director, consortium coordinator | PhD in public law; 25 years in administrative appeals and access to services |
| Youssef El Kaddouri-Lambert | Lead facilitator, 0.8 FTE | Master in law; six years as CPAS appeals officer; organiser in Moroccan-Belgian associations since 2011; Arabic, French, Dutch |
| Oksana Hrytsenko-Dupont | Facilitator, 0.6 FTE | Master in public administration; community organiser with Ukrainian- and Russian-speaking residents since 2022 |
| Lotte Van Gijsegem | Evaluation and data officer, 0.5 FTE | MSc in sociology; questionnaires, response tracker, data protection register |

## 2. Beneficiary: Les Guichets Ouverts (France)

- Association loi 1901, Roubaix (Nord), founded 2013. Nonprofit. Income 2025: EUR 412,000. Staff: 9.
- Activities: accompaniment to public services, digital-access help desk, community mediation.
- Lead: Nadia Tessier-Hamdani, coordinator; master in public law (immigration law); community mediator since 2010.
- GUICHETS role: organised the three Roubaix sessions; tracked responses from the CCAS, the departmental social action service, the family-allowance fund and the municipal education service.

## 3. Beneficiary: Associação Rede Balcão Aberto (Portugal)

- Private nonprofit association, Porto, founded 2016. Income 2025: EUR 236,000. Staff: 7.
- Activities: migrant reception desk, Portuguese conversation circles, mediation with health centres and municipal services.
- Lead: Dr Rita Saldanha Quaresma, PhD in migration sociology (2015, Instituto Superior de Estudos Sociais do Norte). Facilitator: Djibril Có Mendes, community organiser with Bissau-Guinean associations; Portuguese, Kriol, French.
- GUICHETS role: organised the two Porto sessions; led the qualitative strand with officials.

## 4. Agreements

- The CERV consortium agreement for GUICHETS (signed November 2023) covered the project period, which ended on 30 June 2026.
- A framework partnership agreement between the three nonprofits was signed by Seuil Commun on 30 March 2026, Les Guichets Ouverts on 14 April 2026 and Associação Rede Balcão Aberto on 22 April 2026. It runs until 31 December 2029 and covers joint applications in which each organisation acts as a beneficiary, coordination by Seuil Commun, application of participant protocol version 3, and data-sharing terms. No party has given notice; the agreement is in force.

## 5. Associated participant: Cellule Cultures et Quartiers (Liège)

- Municipal cultural office based in Liège; Belgian public body. It runs two neighbourhood cultural spaces, Sainte-Marguerite and Droixhe, with a staff of six programmers and venue managers.
- Letter of association signed by the head of office on 8 July 2026. Contribution in kind: use of the two spaces, up to 12 evening or weekend slots per year, and advice from programming staff.
- Receives no funds and is not a party to the framework partnership agreement. The office holds no heritage collections.

## 6. Facilitators and competences

- 9 facilitators were trained in 2024 (Seuil Commun 4, Les Guichets Ouverts 3, Rede Balcão Aberto 2). Each pairs training in administrative law or public-service procedure with at least three years of practice in migrant community organisations.
- Cultural-space activity to date: one consultation on 13 June 2026 at the Sainte-Marguerite space, held with Cellule Cultures et Quartiers, on use of the space by residents' associations. 23 residents took part, with interpretation in four languages. The session had no heritage content. A summary was sent to the office, which replied in writing on 2 September 2026.
- No staff member of the three partners has training or professional experience in heritage interpretation, museum mediation or participatory work with cultural heritage.
- Planned: on 10 September 2026 the board of Seuil Commun approved a fixed-term 0.5 FTE heritage-mediation officer post (profile: heritage interpretation or museum mediation). The vacancy had not been published by 30 September 2026.
