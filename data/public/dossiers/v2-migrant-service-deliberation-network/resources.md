# Resources, participant protocol and data holdings

Seuil Commun asbl, on behalf of the GUICHETS partners. Status at 30 September 2026.

## 1. Premises and venues

- Seuil Commun, Liège: meeting room for 35 people with step-free access and a childcare corner.
- Les Guichets Ouverts, Roubaix: community hall for 40 people.
- Rede Balcão Aberto, Porto: reception premises for 25 people. The 2025 sessions used a civic-centre room in Bonfim parish, rented per session.
- Two neighbourhood cultural spaces run by Cellule Cultures et Quartiers (Sainte-Marguerite, Droixhe), available under the letter of association of 8 July 2026 (up to 12 slots per year).

## 2. Equipment and tools

- 40 receivers and 6 transmitters for whispered interpretation, owned by Seuil Commun (purchased 2024).
- Recommendation and response tracker: a shared database holding the 41 recommendations of the 2025 cycle, coded with the scheme published in 2026.
- Easy-read templates in French and Portuguese.
- Interpretation: framework contract with the cooperative Interprètes Solidaires Liège until 31 December 2026. Roubaix and Porto book community interpreters per session.

## 3. Participant protocol, version 3

Adopted by the three partners on 3 March 2026; replaces version 2 (January 2025). Item 7 as amended by addendum 1 of 30 September 2026.

1. Written participation consent in the resident's language; participation has no effect on access to any service of the partners.
2. No audio or video recording of deliberation. Notes are taken without names.
3. Case stories are collected under internal-use consent, pseudonymised, and used for internal analysis and training.
4. Publishing an identifiable case story (name, image, voice or recognisable details) requires a separate, written opt-in, obtained in advance in the resident's language and revocable at any time. Refusal has no consequence.
5. Individual legal or residence issues raised in sessions are referred to the advice desks and are not handled in deliberation.
6. Each site has a named safeguarding lead. Incidents are logged and reported to the coordinator within 48 hours.
7. Retention: grant-relevant attendance evidence, including sign-in sheets, is kept securely for five years after the final payment of the grant concerned, or longer while an audit, review or legal proceeding remains open; unnecessary copies are deleted. Case stories are kept for five years.

Identifiable public case-story permissions on file under version 3: 0.

**Addendum 1, adopted by the three partners on 30 September 2026.** Item 7 as adopted on 3 March 2026 read: "Sign-in sheets are destroyed within three months of the cycle report; case stories are kept for five years." That wording did not meet the record-keeping obligations of grant agreement 101143871 and is replaced by the text above. The 2025 source sheets and participant identifiers were prematurely destroyed on 31 March 2026 and cannot be recovered; the incident has been recorded and notified to the granting authority.
