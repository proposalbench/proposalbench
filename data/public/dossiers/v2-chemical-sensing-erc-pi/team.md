## Profiles

**Ulrike Auernig-Sattler.** Principal investigator of CALISORB (ERC-2022-COG, 1 May 2023 – 30 April 2028), 0.5 FTE on the grant. Sets the scientific direction, conceived the reference-stack concept (2019) and the RDC calibration, designed the blinded recovery study with T. Weninger and H. Lindmayr. Inventor on EP26162418.7.

**Jakob Feichtlbauer.** Dr. techn. in Electrical Engineering (optoelectronics), TU Ennstal, 2010. Development engineer for handheld spectrometers at Grazer Spektraltechnik GmbH, Graz, 2010–2014; postdoctoral university assistant, TU Ennstal, 2014–2019; Senior Scientist since March 2019. Leads Task 4.3 (instrumentation) in WP4. Designed and built the portable readers PR2-A and PR2-B (2025), including optics, flow cell, firmware and acquisition software; co-developed the RDC assay protocol with the PI and T. Weninger; operator in the blinded recovery study; co-inventor on EP26162418.7. 19 peer-reviewed articles. Grants as principal investigator: TU Ennstal Innovation Seed Fund 2021, *Low-cost LED reflectometer for porous coatings*, EUR 18,000. Other funding: employed on the PI's FWF Stand-Alone Project (2020–2023) and on CALISORB (since 2023).

**Mariana Teixeira Lobo.** PhD in Materials Chemistry, Universidade do Litoral Norte, Porto, 2021. Coating synthesis, ellipsometric porosimetry and batch reproducibility (WP1); first author of the 2025 article on wafer-scale reproducibility.

**Defne Akyürek.** Surface chemistry and cyclodextrin grafting (WP1); doctoral completion expected in 2027.

**Tobias Weninger.** Signal model and calibration statistics (WP2, WP3); performed the bootstrap and Passing–Bablok analyses of the blinded study; operator in the study; co-inventor on EP26162418.7.

**Sabine Rührlinger.** Coating deposition, wafer dicing and chip production; maintains the chip stock and batch records.
