# Teaching collaboration: partners, personnel and resources

Administrative record closed: 30 September 2026

## Legal membership

The collaboration comprises three independent higher education institutions, each with its own governing body and degree-awarding responsibilities.

| Institution and legal identity | Country and establishment | Charter status |

| Velinac College of Music Pedagogy; Veleučilište za glazbenu pedagogiju Velinac, a Croatian higher education institution with separate legal personality, based in Rijeka | Croatia; established 2005 | Holds an Erasmus Charter for Higher Education for 2021–2027 |
| Ravelbeek University College of Music and Language Education; Hogeschool voor Muziek en Taalonderwijs Ravelbeek vzw, a Belgian nonprofit association based in Leuven | Belgium; established 1996 | Holds an Erasmus Charter for Higher Education for 2021–2027 |

Kaarensalmi's operating licence covers music and music-pedagogy degree programmes.

The institutions signed their teaching collaboration agreement on 17 June 2024. It covers joint staff seminars, development of teaching materials, moderation of assessment and procedures for exchanging approved research extracts. Each legal entity signs its own contracts and controls its own finances.

She reports access problems to the coordination group and maintains a confidential route for students who prefer not to disclose an access need to their assessor. Local programme heads retain responsibility for implementing adjustments.

## Available teaching resources

The collaboration has three existing teaching studios, one at each institution. Each has audio recording equipment, an induction loop and a display for written instructions. Adjustable seating, accessible electronic handouts and captioned teaching clips were used in the completed pilot. The studios remain in ordinary teaching use. Technical support is provided by studio technicians and covers studio recording and caption correction.

All partners have institutionally licensed captioning software available through their teaching systems. Automatic captions require human correction, particularly for musical terminology, overlapping speech and Croatian inflections. Student recordings remain within institutionally controlled storage.

## Placement register and baseline records

At its meeting on 25 September 2026, the coordination group agreed to explore a network of 12 external teaching placements. At the close of this record, no placement agreements have been signed and no placement sites, mentors or students have been recruited for the network.

Historical placement registers were kept separately by each institution. They record hosts, dates and supervisors, but do not identify which placements met a common accessibility definition. The number of existing accessible placements cannot be established from these records.

The student-record systems lack a harmonised disability classification and a compatible participation numerator and denominator. A baseline rate of disabled-student participation cannot be calculated from them. Language-support and caption requests are recorded as service requests.
