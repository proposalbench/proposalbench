# Dr Aino Kaarnevuo

**Principal Lecturer in Music Pedagogy and Director of Teaching Development**  
Kaarensalmi University of Applied Sciences, Tampere, Finland  
CV updated: 21 September 2026

## Education and appointments

I completed a master's degree in music education in 2002 and a doctorate in education in 2010. My doctoral research examined how instrumental teachers communicated assessment criteria during individual lessons, using recorded teaching, stimulated recall and analysis of student work.

I joined Kaarensalmi University of Applied Sciences as a lecturer in 2003, became senior lecturer in 2011 and principal lecturer in 2018. Since 2021 I have also directed teaching development. My responsibilities include staff development, review of practical assessment and coordination of curriculum changes with programme boards. I remain involved in teaching undergraduate instrumental pedagogy and supervising practice-based dissertations.

## Research and teaching responsibilities

My work concerns the relationship between instructions, access arrangements and what students can demonstrate in practical assessments. I use classroom observation, interviews and repeated assessment alongside descriptive and inferential analysis. I have experience integrating these forms of evidence within institutional teaching reviews.

I coordinated the internally funded project Studio Instructions and Shared Access during 2025. I was responsible for the common assessment protocol, reporting to the institutional teaching boards and reconciliation of the project accounts.

In the consortium's signed teaching collaboration, I chair the academic coordination group. Each institution retains responsibility for its own awards, student records, employment arrangements, contracts, finances and curriculum approvals.

## Methodological experience

My practical experience includes rubric development, assessor moderation, paired assessment analysis and reflexive thematic analysis. For the studio pilot, I worked with local teachers to distinguish musical execution from the explanation and adaptation of teaching instructions. I have also used audio annotation to examine pauses and overlapping speech in recorded lessons.

I teach in Finnish and English. My teaching-development work includes consultations with students about access arrangements and revision of practical assessment instructions.
