## Equipment and infrastructure

- **Thermal tolerance laboratory:** Programmable water bath (Julabo FP50-ME, acquired September 2025 with PB-FILATHERM funds), oxygen analyser (Sable Systems FoxBox) for metabolic rate measurements, stereomicroscope, and associated consumables. Located in CESERAL Building B, Room 214.
- **Field infrastructure:** Four permanent 50 × 50 m plots in the Sierra de los Filabres, equipped with iButton temperature/humidity loggers (48 units) and time-lapse cameras (8 units). Access via CESERAL field vehicle.
- **Open-top chambers:** 6 hexagonal open-top warming chambers (1.5 m diameter, 0.4 m height) ordered in August 2026 for deployment at the 950 m and 1,450 m sites in spring 2027. Cost: EUR 8,400 from PB-FILATHERM consumables budget. Chambers have been ordered but not yet deployed.
- **Computing:** Access to CESERAL's Linux cluster (128 cores, 512 GB RAM) for network analyses and null-model permutations.
- **Limitation:** CESERAL does not have a controlled-environment room large enough for whole-community thermal experiments; the current setup handles individual insects only.
