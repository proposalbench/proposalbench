## Research summary

My research focuses on how heat waves alter plant–pollinator network structure and pollination services along elevation gradients in Mediterranean mountains. I combine field sampling of interaction networks with laboratory measurements of insect thermal tolerance (critical thermal maximum, CTmax) and metabolic rates. My current grant (PB-FILATHERM, CESERAL Emerging Groups Programme, reference PB-CESERAL-2025-01) funds a four-site elevation transect in the Sierra de los Filabres (Almería), where my group has recorded 18,742 plant–pollinator interaction events in the first full season and measured CTmax for 64 pollinator species. The first full field season was completed in 2026; the second is planned for 2027.
