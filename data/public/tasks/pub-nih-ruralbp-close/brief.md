# Notes for the proposal

1 October 2026

I'd like to build on the loose end from V-RMAPS: getting a reading into a matched record doesn't tell us whether anyone can use it to arrange follow-up. The next question is where that process breaks when measurements originate outside the clinic, and which failures residents and staff think are worth fixing first.

Please use the linkage paper, interview findings and paper-form exercise as starting points. The improvement between the two staff sessions is encouraging, but the same people used the form twice. I'd like fresh usability evidence and a clearer account of failed matches and missing measurement context.

Keep this manageable: approach two of the partner clinics, one in each state, about community measurement sessions and record reconciliation. We could recruit 60 adults for paired community and clinic measurements using our validated monitors and a documented protocol. Explore transport, telephone access and medication-cost uncertainty without assuming our earlier interviews tell us how common those problems are. Include people who struggle to attend appointments, rather than relying entirely on regular attenders.

I'd like three years: the first for permissions, interviews and measurement work; the second for linkage checks and co-design; and the third for a randomised pilot. For that pilot, assign the 60 adults equally to the new follow-up workflow or usual care and compare standardized systolic blood pressure after six months. That would give us an initial estimate of whether the strategy lowers pressure.

Ask the advisory group to help choose priorities and revise the workflow in separate working sessions. Tessavelle can lead that work, with Naomelle handling linkage procedures and Ruthavelle coordinating community involvement. Staff time and clinic space need negotiating. New contact, measurements and record access need their own permissions; the old award is closed. Please keep the workflow simple enough to use when broadband is unreliable.
