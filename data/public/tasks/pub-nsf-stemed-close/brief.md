# Notes for the proposal writer

Date: 1 October 2026

Here's what I'm thinking for this NSF call. We want to build directly on the RatioWeave-Tutor RCT we did with Spencer. The big finding was that high-dosage tutoring works, but we still don't understand *why* it works, which tutoring interactions actually drive the learning gains, and whether the same mechanisms operate for different student groups. That's the fundamental research question the call is after.

The idea: we go back into CQMSD middle schools (we have the MOU through 2028) and do a mixed-methods study that combines a new quasi-experimental design with intensive video analysis of tutoring sessions. We'd look at how specific features of tutor–student mathematical dialogue — things like tutor questioning moves, student explanation quality, use of multiple representations — predict gains in algebraic reasoning and transfer to science problem-solving contexts. Marcus would lead the qualitative/interaction analysis side, I'd handle the quantitative design and causal modelling. Yunara Pellen is perfect for the multilevel work.

We want to focus on grades 7–8, where the algebra content is richest and where we can follow students' explanations and transfer across connected topics. We'd recruit about 20 schools, half treatment half comparison, and do deep video observation in a purposive subsample of maybe 8 schools.

Practical notes:
- I'd like this to be a **5-year project** so we have time for two full cohorts of students plus a year for instrument development and a final year for analysis and dissemination.
- Budget should be close to the cap — around $1.4M total.
- For Results from Prior NSF Support, use our Spencer-funded RatioWeave-Tutor study as the award to report under both my name and Marcus's; it is our most relevant prior grant. Highlight the algebraic-reasoning finding.
- Make sure the broader impacts section talks about our existing relationship with CQMSD and how findings will feed back to the district's tutoring programme design.
- We need to be upfront about the single-district limitation and frame the boundary analysis carefully.

Let me know if you need anything else from the dossier files.
