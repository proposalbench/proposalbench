1 October 2026, Sorcha

We're taking OUTFALERT to PB2-HE-REV-WATER-02. Same three partners: CICS, URC and WISW, with the MoU signed on 18 September. Still 36 months, still Galway Bay and the Charente mouth. I'd go to about EUR 4.2 million this time, mainly for the two-estuary sampling campaign.

**Keep**

The warning chain (nowcasts + telemetry + TELEMAC travel times, shadow mode for two seasons), the hindcast, WISW's planning link and the preregistration, which the reviewers liked.

Then spend the words we save on answering the reviewers point by point. 18,000 words will be tight with everything else we have to add.

All the background is in the technical update of 30 September. Please don't overstate the pilot.
