# Notes for proposal writer

Hi — here's what I'm thinking for the MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship call.

## The idea

I want to pivot from my usual mixed-methods trial work toward **causal-inference methods for evaluating maternal health programmes at scale**. Specifically, I'd apply quasi-experimental techniques (difference-in-differences with staggered adoption, synthetic control) to the NEVRAMAT dataset and the new NWO-WOTRO scale-up data, combined with the NCHRI demographic surveillance data, to estimate the causal effect of CHW-led PPH screening on maternal outcomes across multiple districts. This is a genuine gap in my profile — I flagged it in my training self-assessment — and it's exactly what Lieve Janssens-Willekens's group at VIPH in Antwerp specialises in. The fellowship would let me learn these methods properly and apply them to my own data.

The broader contribution: moving beyond the single stepped-wedge trial to produce policy-relevant causal estimates that Ghana Health Service can use for national scale-up decisions, plus a cost-effectiveness component I've never done.

## Host and practical setup

I'd like **DU in Maastricht as the host institution** — Hendrel Vossenkade knows my work, the department has the data infrastructure, and I'm already embedded there. Lieve at VIPH could be involved through a secondment of maybe 6 months for the causal-inference training. And I'd want a secondment at NCHRI in Ghana (3–4 months) for fieldwork and data linkage with the HDSS.

Duration: 24 months total.

- NWO-WOTRO pilot data (410 women, 6 zones, 3 districts — will be complete by fellowship start)
- NCHRI HDSS data for the synthetic control approach
