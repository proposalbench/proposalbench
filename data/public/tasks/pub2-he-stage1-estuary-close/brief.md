This is the PB-BUURTGETIJ-26 follow-up we talked about in Oldenburg, with WEIH and FBMI as full beneficiaries this time. It's stage 1, so just Excellence and Impact. The work plan and money come at stage 2.

**The idea**

- Run the PB-BUURTGETIJ-26 chain (WEIH river ensemble → SWMM/2D → block exposure → who is exposed → options) in three towns instead of one:
  - Dordrecht.
  - Leer on the Ems/Leda, where WEIH has gauges and a TELEMAC set-up.
  - A Danish Wadden Sea town with FBMI (Ejnar suggests Ribe).
  
  Dordrecht is dominated by the tidal river and the other two are more surge-driven. That gives us the contrast for transferability.
- Fix the weak points from section 6 of the final report: couple river levels dynamically instead of imposing them, add surge, and get validation closer to big events.
