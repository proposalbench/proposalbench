# Notes from Annick: heritage topic

*1 October 2026*

Same three organisations under our 2026 framework agreement, Seuil Commun coordinating. Same GUICHETS method, but residents come in through heritage instead of service procedures.

**The idea**

- Oral-history evenings, neighbourhood walks and a co-curated display (arrival stories, old workplaces, street names, markets), then deliberation at language tables and a public written answer, as in GUICHETS.
- Three communes next to our offices, where many advice-desk clients live: Clairvérive (Liège area), Filandrecourt (Roubaix area), Vale Serenilho (Porto area). Venues: I'm approaching Clairvérive's library, Nadia the Filandrecourt médiathèque, Rita a parish centre in Vale Serenilho; possible associated partners. The Cellule Cultures et Quartiers slots (letter of 8 July) can host the joint exchange and training.

**Decisions** (each council is competent; written confirmations in September, emails to follow)

- Clairvérive: reuse of its vacant former municipal school. The conseil communal decides, not before October 2029. On 24 September the collège confirmed a reasoned written response within 60 days of receiving proposals, presented at a public meeting.
- Filandrecourt: names for the new streets and square on the former mill site; conseil municipal vote planned late 2029. The deputy mayor's office confirmed on 17 September a written response within three months, on the town website before the vote.
- Vale Serenilho: reuse of the vacant municipal workshop. The Câmara Municipal decides in 2029; its culture department confirmed on 29 September a public written reply within 60 days.

**What to build on**

- Facilitators, interpretation in 11 languages, the receivers, childcare, the response tracker and protocol v3.
- We reached 96 residents across the eight 2025 sessions (attendance consolidation). Use that as proven capacity and size targets from it: roughly 100–120 residents a year across the three localities.
- 33 of 41 recommendations answered, the knowledge-card gain, the policy brief.
- For the display and an online collection, start from our case-story archive (52 records, 2024–2026). Pick the strongest; many are already migration stories. Residents add their own on top.

**Gaps and practicalities**

- Nobody in the three teams has heritage training. The board approved a 0.5 FTE heritage-mediation officer on 10 September; vacancy out this autumn.
- EU level: READ (I'm on its board) plus whatever EU process fits. GUICHETS never reached EU level; this time it must.
- 24 months from March 2028, close to the EUR 300,000 ceiling.
- Saturdays and evenings again; we missed under-25s and evening-shift workers last time.
