# Notes for the PoC proposal

1 October 2026, Ulrike

**Idea.** Get RDC and the PR2 readers out of our lab and find out whether staff at utilities and testing labs can use them as a diclofenac screen that decides which samples go to LC-MS/MS. The May workshop pointed there. Nobody expects a compliance method from us: 6 of 12 said compliance needs accredited or standardised methods. But 7 of 12 would use a screen to pick samples for confirmation.

**Build on.**
- The blinded study: 36 samples, 94% recovery overall, Passing–Bablok slope 0.94 against Herbert's LC-MS/MS. Dataset 4417 is public.
- PR2-A and PR2-B, the 132 chips in stock, and the acquisition/RDC code.
- The workshop contacts (Irene at the TTO holds the list).
- Herbert's LC-MS/MS for the confirmations (EUR 38 per injection).

What we have never done: unspiked real samples, other sites, and operators who didn't build the reader. That is the point of the PoC.

**People.** Jakob should be the PI on this one. He built both readers and spent four years on handheld spectrometers at Grazer Spektraltechnik. With the deputy-head job I can't run another project day to day. He can free about a third of his time from the university-funded part of his post. I'd stay in at around 10% as the ERC grant holder behind it. Mariana leaves in August 2027, so don't plan her in.

**IP.** The RDC method is covered by our European patent EP26162418.7, granted and owned by TU Ennstal, so an instrument maker would be protected from the start. Please make that the main argument for the licence route and open licence talks early.

**Route.** I want two options kept open until the end:
- a licence to an instrument company;
- the calibration service I proposed to the Rectorate. They want a cost model and a market assessment before deciding, and the PoC could produce both.

**Keep out.** Carbamazepine/sulfamethoxazole calibration and the antifouling coatings are still CALISORB work.
