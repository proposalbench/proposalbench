# Notes from Aino

1 October 2026

I'd like this application to take our studio teaching sequence into supervised teaching placements while improving how we use it in the three existing studios. Keep music pedagogy at the centre: students should practise explaining a rehearsal task, recognising an access obstacle and adapting their instructions without losing the musical purpose.

Build on the written and spoken instructions, corrected captions, comprehension checks and moderated rubric from the 2025 pilot. The interviews give us useful starting points, especially the difficulty of reading captions while playing. Bring students with sensory or language access barriers into the design sessions and pay attention to the option of separating viewing from playing. Please keep musical execution separate from the pedagogical assessment.

Use our operational network of 12 teaching placements as the delivery backbone. Describe it as capacity we can draw on from the start, with supervisors joining the studio teachers for training and moderation. I'd like the same core materials to work across studios and placements, with local adaptations where teaching conditions differ.

Petra should lead moderation, Lotte should organise the programme-board work, and Maija should lead language adaptation with local teachers and students. Eeva needs time to review evaluation access and confidential adjustment requests. Plan around teaching semesters over 30 months; staff release and caption correction need realistic allocations.

Leave out a separate impact and dissemination section—the studios and placement activities will speak for themselves, and I'd rather use the space for delivery details. The practical endpoint is a teaching pack that departments keep using after the project. For the evaluation, build on the pilot honestly and make follow-up easier to complete; losing 12 of the 72 assessments left us with questions we still cannot answer.
