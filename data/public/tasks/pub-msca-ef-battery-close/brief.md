1 October 2026

I'd like to take the SULVERNEX resistance result into a project that can distinguish structural change from loss of contact during cycling. The Swedish group looks like the right setting: please build this around Sörnelqvist as the proposed supervisor at the Sörvaskel Institute of Materials Science in Uppsala. We already work together, and learning to design and analyse operando experiments would be a substantial next step for me.

Keep the coated versus uncoated LiNi0.8Mn0.1Co0.1O2/Li6PS5Cl comparison as the starting point. Please describe the 2026 SULVERNEX paper as published in the experience section; use its title and the DOI in the register. It is the clearest account of the experiment I want to build on.

I suggest 30 months overall, so there is time for operando training, repeat preparations and a final analysis and writing period. Start with the Swedish group's existing laboratory equipment and make synchrotron measurements conditional on an access award. Arvenstald would be a useful contact for planning that application.

I can bring the sample manifests, fitting scripts and batch-resampling workflow into the host group. In return, I need supervised practice in cell alignment, diffraction analysis and experimental design. I'd also like a supported first teaching activity and some experience mentoring a student, if the host can arrange these. My aim is to become ready to lead a small experimental programme.
