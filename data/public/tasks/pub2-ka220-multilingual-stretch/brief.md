# Notes from Aino

1 October 2026

I'd like to use this application to move our studio work into acoustic language-model evaluation. The central output I have in mind is a public leaderboard showing which models handle multilingual rehearsal instructions best: overlapping voices, musical terminology, pauses and corrections. We could build a shared test collection from newly recreated teaching examples in Finnish, Croatian, Dutch and English, then score transcription accuracy, terminology errors and whether a model preserves the intended instruction. I'd organise the main work packages around collection design, model comparison and publishing reproducible rankings. The leaderboard should be useful independently of any particular course; our existing teaching seminars could introduce it to colleagues.

Build on the instruction sequence, terminology sheets, assessment rubrics and audio annotation from Studio Instructions and Shared Access. What interests me now is whether acoustic analysis can help us understand where spoken studio instructions become difficult to follow.

The three institutions would remain beneficiaries, with Kaarensalmi coordinating. Maija can lead language adaptation and Eeva can oversee accessibility. We'll need to hire someone with speech-model evaluation experience. Local teachers would need release time to contribute examples and interpret errors.

I still need the local teams to supply exact module titles, teaching languages and learner-language profiles, including counts and languages used. The terminology sheets aren't enough to fill those gaps. For now, use labelled placeholders or refer to the music-pedagogy modules where the instruction sequence was approved. I also need confirmation of how Kaarensalmi and Ravelbeek would recognise staff training; leave those arrangements marked for confirmation.

Plan for 30 months and the EUR 250,000 option. Use the existing studios and licensed teaching systems. Teacher release time is the practical constraint I most want the allocation to address.
