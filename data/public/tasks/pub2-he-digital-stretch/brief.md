# Notes from Mireille

1 October 2026

I’d like this application to move us from classifying laboratory food measurements into AI that makes useful decisions when industrial sensor streams become unreliable. Build on Mésurafroid-25: the paired measurements, batch-held-out analysis, documented missingness and replay scripts give us a practical starting point. Please keep the distinction between surrogate-growth prediction and demonstrated benefits in operations.

My preferred centrepiece is a pick-and-place controller for crate handling in a small robotics cell we would set up in the Ghent pilot room. It would replan movements when position messages disappear or get replayed. A demonstration with dummy loads would let us schedule the engineering work without waiting for commercial site access; our experience with telemetry faults gives us a starting point for specifying the tests. Make cycle time, positioning error and recovery time the main success measures. Keep the chilled-food work as the motivating example; I would leave shelf-life and food-loss measurements for a later application so that this project has a manageable engineering focus.

For the consortium, I suggest leaving Kelderspoor out of the new funding request and keeping their completed replay work as background. Nantes, the Polish university and our Ghent branch would give us the French, Polish and Belgian participants. Put Ghent on its own participant row and give it responsibility for integration demonstrations; that would also make better use of the pilot room.

We need new machine-learning and robotics expertise, plus someone in human factors to work with staff on explanations and overrides. Treat those as proposed appointments or partnerships: nobody has agreed yet. We can also approach a chilled-food distributor for operational input, but there is no access agreement to cite.

Aim for 42 months and around EUR 4.6 million. Our existing staff allocations are already committed, so distinguish newly funded effort from current national work. Allow time for recruitment, partner agreements and access negotiations before demonstrations.
