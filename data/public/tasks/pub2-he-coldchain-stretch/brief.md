We are pivoting our secure robotics and data pipeline work to target this new food cold-chain call. We want to combine TUF's resilient decision-support systems and ALV's robotics with IMAA's microbial spoilage models and AKK's logistics network.

Here is the core idea: we will use our secure edge-computing pipelines to ingest continuous environmental data from AKK's warehouses and ALV's mobile platforms. IMAA will build predictive models on how spoilage organisms behave when the cold chain breaks.

To really tackle the spoilage issue at the source, IMAA should lead a work package to develop a new chemical food preservative that can be applied to the food before transport. This will be our main intervention to reduce spoilage during temperature abuse.

For the validation phase at AKK's Innsbruck facility, we want to test our new cold-chain policies by directly taking over and controlling their live refrigeration systems. This will let us prove our decision-support tool works in a real commercial environment.

Since the call asks for social sciences to look at adoption barriers, and we don't have that in the consortium, please include a plan to recruit an SSH postdoc at TUF or bring in a specialized subcontractor to handle the behavioral and organizational aspects.

Let's aim for a 40-month duration and request EUR 5.4 million.
