The question I have in mind is whether we can recover note-onset patterns from our contemporary performance examples when short stretches of audio are missing, without introducing convincing events that were never in the recording. Use the 24 existing examples from eight performers, keeping the untouched recordings as references. Our 24 examples are unaccompanied solo singing with underlaid text. There are no reference onset annotations yet. Start by checking which vocal note attacks can be identified reliably and create checked annotations, retaining ambiguous attacks as uncertain.

Compare an adapted recovery method with a simple baseline and combine numerical error measures with close inspection of musically consequential failures. These recordings can test recovery of a modern performance; they cannot establish how the manuscripts originally sounded.
