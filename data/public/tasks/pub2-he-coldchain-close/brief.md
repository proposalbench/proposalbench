# Notes from Mireille

1 October 2026

I want to build directly on Mésurafroid-25: paired chemical measurements, temperature histories and microbial reference counts, with telemetry checks feeding an advisory decision tool. Keep rice and poultry as the starting matrices; we know the calibration problems there.

The next question is what happens during repeated interruptions across storage and transport. I want uncertainty around the growth predictions that operators can use when deciding whether to hold a lot or take another sample. The connector failures during excursion handling still bother me. We should budget time to work through those conditions, including what the tool displays when a sensor history is incomplete.

I suggest requesting EUR 6.2 million from the EU over 42 months. That would cover additional microbiology staff, sensor integration, commercial demonstrations and an adoption study. The current national awards already occupy our staff.

For the packing demonstration, LL-07 is our nonpathogenic surrogate, so let's run inoculated research lots through a producer's packing line under BS-IIMAV-24-06 and withdraw them from sale. That would connect the measurements to actual packing operations.

We still need operator access agreements. Start those conversations early with producers, logistics providers and retailers. I'd like food safety authorities involved in discussing sampling and interpreting alerts. We also need someone with SSH experience to look at workload and why staff override recommendations. Keep the interface advisory. Leave enough time at the end for independent validation and operator training.
