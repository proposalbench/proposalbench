# Notes for the proposal (Annick)

*1 October 2026*

The idea is a second GUICHETS round. The core format stays: an interpreted briefing by the service, language tables, recommendations agreed in plenary, written responses and feedback meetings. This time we fix what went wrong in 2025 and finally get residents' voices to Brussels.

What to build on:

- Lotte's response tracker and the coding scheme from the 2026 paper. Eight recommendations never got an answer. This time someone has to chase them.
- The pre/post cards on complaint routes worked. The exit questionnaire worked less well, and we couldn't link anything to attendance.
- We almost entirely missed under-25s and people working evening shifts.
- READ: I'm on the board, and they can set up a meeting with Commission and Parliament people at the end. We made no EU-level contribution last time.

Shape:

- Keep it in Liège this time. Nadia and Rita both say their teams are thin for 2028. One city also means one interpreter contract, no travel, and more assemblies for the same money.
- Cellule Cultures et Quartiers can be the second beneficiary instead. They signed the letter in July, and the Sainte-Marguerite and Droixhe spaces are much better for evening and weekend sessions than our room. That covers the two-partner rule, and READ gives us the European side.
- Droixhe in particular, for the young people and the shift workers.
- 24 months, the full EUR 300,000.
- Visibility: I'd like a public booklet and a small travelling exhibition, "Au guichet", with real residents' stories: first name, a photo and a few lines in their own words. We already have 52 case stories in the archive, and everyone signed a consent form when they joined a session. So it's mostly a matter of picking the strongest ones. Officials and MEPs respond to faces, not percentages.

Practical:

- Youssef and Oksana facilitate. The two jurists stay on the advice clinic and only take referrals.
- The Interprètes Solidaires contract ends in December 2026, so cost interpretation from scratch.
- Lotte keeps the data protection register as before.
