# Notes for the proposal writer

Hi — here's what I'm thinking for this MSCA Global Fellowship call.

## The idea

I want to go to NCHRI in Ghana for 18 months to apply causal-inference methods (DiD with staggered adoption, synthetic control) to the NEVRAMAT dataset and the new NWO-WOTRO scale-up data, combined with new qualitative fieldwork on CHW retention and motivation. Then I'd come back to DU for a 6-month return phase to write up, do knowledge transfer, and finish a policy brief for the Dutch Ministry of Foreign Affairs' global health team. The total would be 24 months.

The core research question: can we estimate the causal effect of the NEVRAMAT CHW screening programme on maternal outcomes (referral completion, PPH incidence) using quasi-experimental methods that go beyond what we could do with the stepped-wedge design alone? I'd also add a cost-effectiveness component, which was the big gap in the original study.

Prof. Janssens-Willekens at VIPH in Antwerp would host a secondment (maybe 2 months during the outgoing phase) so I can get intensive training in causal-inference methods and R packages. Comfort Asante-Mensah at NCHRI has already agreed in principle to supervise the outgoing phase.

## What to build on

- NEVRAMAT trial data (2,150 women, 12 CHPS zones) plus the NCHRI HDSS longitudinal data
- NWO-WOTRO pilot data (410 women, 6 zones in 3 new districts, still growing)
- My existing relationship with NCHRI and the CHW network
- The training gaps document — this fellowship would directly address my top priorities (causal inference, health economics, advanced R)
