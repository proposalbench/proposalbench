# Notes from the Coordinator
**Date: 1 October 2026**

Hi team,

This CERV call is exactly what we need to scale up the Civic Heritage Network. It perfectly supports our strategic shift from informal cultural gatherings to formal service-access deliberations. Let's propose a 24-month project starting in early 2028 across our three nodes (Palermo, Athens, Valencia), requesting around EUR 280,000. 

We will use our musical heritage sharing method to bring in excluded residents—specifically those facing severe language and migration barriers—and then transition into structured deliberations. To make sure the authorities actually listen, we will finally contract our planned public-service specialists, Matteo Zanfretta and Sofia Rinalvesi, to translate the residents' proposals into formal administrative demands.
