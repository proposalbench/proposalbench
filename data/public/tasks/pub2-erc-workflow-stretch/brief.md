# Notes for the PoC proposal

*Ulrike, 1 October 2026*

Please have a full draft ready by Christmas, so Irene (TTO) and the Rectorate can comment.

**Idea.** I want to move from "a sensor for diclofenac" to a screening step that treatment plants run themselves. Under the recast Urban Waste Water Treatment Directive, larger plants must add a fourth treatment stage and show removal of indicator substances, and diclofenac is one of them. Operators would measure before and after that stage with a PR2-type reader. They would send only flagged or borderline samples to an accredited LC-MS/MS lab. That matches what we heard at the workshop: turnaround and cost per sample are the problem, and 7 of 12 would use screening to pick samples for confirmation. What we don't know is whether RDC holds up on site, in unspiked effluent, when it is run by people who didn't build the reader.

**Build on.** RDC (EP26162418.7), the blinded study and record 4417, PR2-A/B, and the 132 chips in stock. Diclofenac only. Carbamazepine, mixtures and antifouling stay in CALISORB until April 2028.

**Service.** The TU Ennstal Trace Residue Calibration Service is up and running in all but name. The three utilities from May are our first clients, so we can present them as paying users and run the user-validation milestones with them. End point: either a proper university unit or a licence to one of the testing labs. The PoC should tell us which.

**Fish.** At the workshop a utility engineer asked whether a reading above our threshold means anything for fish. I'd like a small add-on: expose juvenile rainbow trout to effluent spiked at the screening thresholds and score gill and kidney histology. LP-OA-07 already covers spiked effluent and the reference standards, so Sabine can run it in building C around months 6–10.
