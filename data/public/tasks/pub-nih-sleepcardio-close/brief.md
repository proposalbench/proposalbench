Let's go after this R21. In our LAPSES cohort, we clearly saw that sleep regularity drops during early adolescence and is linked to higher BMI z-scores. Now it's time to take the next step and look at broader cardiometabolic risk, specifically blood pressure, which we haven't measured before.

Since we have great partnerships with the local FQHCs, we want to run a 6-week sleep-extension intervention. We'll recruit 60 rural adolescents and prospectively assign half to a behavioral sleep-coaching program and the other half to a waitlist control. We will use our ActiGraph GT9X Links to measure their sleep timing and regularity, and we'll partner with the clinic nurses to measure their blood pressure before and after the intervention to see if improving sleep regularity lowers cardiometabolic risk.

This is a new direction for our lab since we haven't done cardiovascular assessments or interventions before, so it fits the exploratory R21 mechanism perfectly. It will give us the feasibility data we need to see if we can actually change sleep behavior before we pitch a massive R01.
