# Notes from Maarja

1 October 2026

Please draft a two-to-three-page concept note building on the Fragmentary Music Evidence Pilot. I would like to keep this within our musical manuscripts and the three-person team at Rannakaar University.

My idea is a cataloguing and digitisation project using the same 180 fragments. We could consolidate the existing images, digitise the associated working annotations and make the catalogue easier to navigate between a fragment, its folder and the reasons recorded for its classification. The 34 disputed fragments would make a useful centrepiece: put the alternative readings alongside one another so that readers can follow the disagreement. I think a complete, usable digital edition is enough of an endpoint here; we can leave testing the interpretations for a later project.

Please build on the annotation guide and July 2026 paper. Keep the original independent labels recoverable. I do not want the presentation to turn the disputed cases into mistakes or imply that agreement established the correct reading.

Plan around 18 months and a foundation request of EUR 80,000 including indirect costs. Use our existing facilities and allow for funded staff time, collection support and repository preparation. I would lead the source work, Tõnis would handle the verbal evidence, and Kadri would manage scripts and record links. Keep the material within the completed pilot holdings and their existing access conditions; no new performers or recordings are needed. The immediate audience is manuscript researchers and collection staff who need to understand how an entry was assembled.
