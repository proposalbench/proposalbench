# Notes for the proposal

1 October 2026 — all dossier statuses still hold.

I want to build directly on CALDERVANE Ireland and follow renovation through to health outcomes with a stronger longitudinal design. The winter temperature findings were useful, but the wheeze estimate was inconclusive. We need enough repeated observations to distinguish renovation effects from weather, changes in occupancy and differences between households selected for works.

Use the completed Irish cohort as our methodological foundation. For the starting-capacity section, present the 400-dwelling extension as already recruited and ready for baseline visits; the June ethics approval gives us a concrete starting point. Keep those households separate from the completed study when describing previous findings.

The new work should follow older residents and children with asthma through renovation, with contemporaneous comparison homes and repeated seasonal measurements. Please connect bedroom overheating, ventilation and dampness to validated health measures. We should improve clinical assessment completion, drawing on the nurse and physician, while keeping home visits manageable for families. Include a sample-size justification that accounts for household clustering and attrition.

Keep the existing five institutions together. Develop prospective fieldwork roles with the Portuguese team, use the Slovenian expertise for building assessment and predictive modelling, and involve the Belgian federation in tenant engagement and discussions with housing providers. The Italian team can support telemetry and data quality. Interviews about ventilation routines, energy costs and maintenance need to inform the analysis and practical guidance.

I suggest requesting EUR 6.4 million in EU funding so we can cover clinical visits, replacement monitoring equipment and participant expenses across 48 months. Cost staff time explicitly: the current Irish allocations are already occupied.

I would like usable ventilation-handover guidance and draft health criteria for renovation passports, developed with practitioners and residents. Build in prospective engagement with public authorities and relevant Cluster 5 projects. Keep identifiable health records under controlled access, with shareable code and documentation.
