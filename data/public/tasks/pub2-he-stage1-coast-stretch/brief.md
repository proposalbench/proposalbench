# Notes for the outline (Geertje, 1 October 2026)

Let's go for this one with WEIH and FBMI. I want to take PB-BUURTGETIJ-26 beyond three neighbourhoods.

**Core idea.** A layout optimisation method for housing in delta towns. For a given site it searches block layouts, street profiles, green space and building heights. It returns the configurations with the fewest exposed households and the fairest spread of benefits. We already have the ingredients:

- the 1D–2D model;
- the 0.30 m entrance threshold;
- the distributional regression;
- the option comparison (green-blue retrofit gave 31% fewer exposed addresses).

Mirthe's methods are the starting point. She submits in May 2027 and her contract ends in August, so she won't be on the project herself.

**Something visible.** The Drechtsteden working group keeps asking for something they can see. I'd make this the main practical work package:

- Koen and WEIH design a permanent flood wall with gated outfalls along the river edge of N2. That is where our model shows the outfalls blocking at high water.
- We build a first 400 m section with the municipality during the project.

That's our showcase for Impact.

**Also build on**
- WEIH: Ems and Weser runoff models, TELEMAC set-ups, the 50-member river ensemble.

- Our floodwater pilot: 22 of 31 samples above 1,000 MPN/100 mL.
- The two-round co-design format; 72% of residents put the green-blue option first.
