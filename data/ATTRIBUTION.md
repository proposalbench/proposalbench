# Attribution

ProposalBench task data are released under CC BY 4.0 (`LICENSE-DATA`). The calls are fictional,
but their structure, evaluation criteria and conditions are adapted from the official documents
below. Per-document rights are audited in `data/seeds/manifest.yaml`. Which seed informed which
task is kept private until the split concerned is published.

## Funders' documents

- **European Commission.** Horizon Europe topic texts retrieved from the EU Funding & Tenders
  Portal (search API), and the Horizon Europe General Annexes 2026–2027. © European Union,
  reused under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU (CC BY 4.0). Changes: topics rewritten, identifiers,
  budgets and conditions invented.
- **European Commission, other programmes.** Horizon Europe stage-1 application form (V5.0);
  Erasmus+ Programme Guide 2026 (cooperation partnerships) and the 2026 KA220-HED call; CERV 2026
  call document CERV-2026-CITIZENS-CIV and the CERV application form; ERC Work Programme 2027 and
  the ERC Proof of Concept information for applicants. © European Union, reused under Commission
  Decision 2011/833/EU (CC BY 4.0). Changes: summarised and paraphrased; topics, identifiers,
  budgets, deadlines and the restrictions each call labels as fictional invented.
- **Agencia Estatal de Investigación (Spain).** Resolution of 29 October 2025 of the call for
  Proyectos de Generación de Conocimiento 2025 (annexes I, III and VII). Official act published in
  the BOE; official acts are excluded from copyright protection (Ley de Propiedad Intelectual,
  art. 13). Changes: summarised and adapted; thematic emphasis invented.
- **National Institutes of Health (US).** PA-25-304 (R21 parent announcement), NIH page-limit and
  format-attachment rules, the resubmission policy and the Simplified Review Framework. Works of
  the US federal government (17 U.S.C. §105). Changes: summarised; research emphasis invented.
- **National Science Foundation (US).** CAREER solicitation NSF 22-586 and the Proposal & Award
  Policies & Procedures Guide (PAPPG 24-1). Works of the US federal government (17 U.S.C. §105).
  Changes: the CAREER-style calls are fictional solicitations modelled on these rules; ceiling,
  emphasis and deadline invented.

The two foundation concept-note calls model no real funder: every condition is invented.
